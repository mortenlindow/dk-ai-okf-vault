# Summary

One of fourteen politically set themes that Danmarks Frie Forskningsfond awards in 2026, financed
by the annual political agreements on the research reserve. The theme funds research on using AI
in cancer care — early detection, diagnostics, tailored treatment and decision support — with a
close link to the health service as a central element. Applications are assessed by an ad hoc
committee for the theme [1][2].

# Facts

| | |
|---|---|

| Awards | 7 projects from 29 applications *(as of 2026-10-05)* |
| Date | awards announced 5 October 2026 |
| Status | Held — 2026 round awarded |

# Notes

DFF's announcement notes that some themes are multi-year and awarded again in 2027–2029; whether
this one recurs is not yet recorded in this vault. Four of the seven awards are hosted by regions [1][2].
