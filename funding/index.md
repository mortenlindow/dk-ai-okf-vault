# Funding programmes

Named pots of money with rules, a call, or an allocation decision.

The three-way cut: the **funder** is an [organisation](../organisations/funders/index.md);
the **programme** is a concept here; a **grant is not an entity** — it is a row in a
programme's `# Awards` table and a `Funded by` link on the recipient.

Where an instrument is both a text and a pot (a tripartite agreement, say), model the pot
here and cite the text. Promote the text to a [policy](../policy/index.md) concept only
when two or more concepts need it for its content rather than its money.

* [Aftale om tre storskalaprojekter med kunstig intelligens i den offentlige sektor](offentlig-ai-aftale-2026.md) - DKK 266.7m tripartite agreement from 2026; one of its three projects lands in clinical practice.

*Expected to grow to include the Novo Nordisk Foundation's AI programmes, Innovationsfonden,
and Nordic and EU instruments.*
