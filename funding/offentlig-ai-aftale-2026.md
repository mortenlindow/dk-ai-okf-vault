# Summary

An agreement between the **Danish government, KL and Danske Regioner** allocating
**DKK 266.7m**, financed jointly by the state, the municipalities and the regions, to
**three large-scale AI projects** rolled out across the public sector from 2026.

It is the largest concrete public-sector AI commitment captured in this vault, and one of
its three projects lands directly in clinical practice.

# Awards

| Project | Scope |
|---|---|
| Automated documentation | AI-assisted documentation using speech-to-text for journals and meeting notes. Starts in employment services and in **clinical practice in the regions**, then extends to health and elderly care in the municipalities. |
| Digital assistant for citizens and businesses | Conversational assistants replacing static self-service solutions. |
| Digital assistants and decision support for employees | Case processing and professional guidance across the public sector. |

*This source gives the total; per-project amounts are not yet recorded in this vault.*

# Notes

The **health-relevant project is the first**: automated documentation in clinical practice
in the regions. This vault records the *agreement and its governance*, not the tools the
projects produce — those are out of scope under the Structure Test.

Parties will revisit the projects' potential in connection with the **2027 economy
agreements**, and legal frameworks for ethical responsibility and data protection are being
developed in parallel.

This instrument is **both a text and a pot**. Per `CLAUDE.md` §1 the pot is modelled here
and the text is cited; a separate `Policy` concept is created only if two or more concepts
come to need the text for its content rather than its money.
