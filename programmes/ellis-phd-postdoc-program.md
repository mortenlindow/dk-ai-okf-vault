# Summary

> **Foreign-run programme — thin entry.** Run by the pan-European [ELLIS](/initiatives/ellis.md)
> network and admitted under the Denmark Test because a Danish unit takes part. It is
> recorded as a node in the Danish landscape, not parsed as a research subject.

A PhD and postdoc programme that *"pairs outstanding students with leading researchers
across Europe in machine learning, AI and related fields."* Its defining structural
feature is **dual supervision spanning two European countries**, together with a
six-month research exchange and a single centralised application.

It connects two mapped concepts — ELLIS runs it, and
[ELLIS Unit Denmark](/initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md) lists it among the activities it
contributes to — and it is itself a form of cross-institutional organising: each doctorate is
designed around supervision in two countries.

# Activities

Three study tracks, cross-sector collaboration, and a six-month research exchange within
Europe. A single centralised application reaches 50–100 institutions *(as of 2026-09-26)*. The current call closes on **2026-10-31**, for entry in 2027.
