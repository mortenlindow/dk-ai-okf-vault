# Programmes

Courses of study whose **subject is AI or machine learning** *and* which **connect two or
more concepts** — offered jointly by several institutions or faculties, or run by a mapped
initiative. **Rare by design.**

This class is the one exception to the Structure Test (`CLAUDE.md` §1), on this reasoning:
**education *organising* is structure; education *provision* is a catalogue someone else
already keeps.** Single-provider AI degrees are recorded as rows in their provider's
`# Activities`, and the complete national list is at
[UddannelsesGuiden](../references/uddannelsesguiden.md).

* [ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program](ellis-phd-postdoc-program.md) - pan-European PhD and postdoc programme in ML and AI, with dual supervision across two countries; Danish participation through ELLIS Unit Denmark.

## Recorded as rows, not files

* **Machine learning og datavidenskab** (UCPH bachelor) - on the [Faculty of Science](../organisations/academic/science-ku.md). Single faculty and single study board, confirmed from its study regulations.
* **Kognitions- og datavidenskab**, AI specialisation (UCPH bachelor) - on [SAMF](../organisations/academic/samf-ku.md).
* **PhD cohort** across five universities - on the [Pioneer Centre for AI](../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md). Not a named programme.

## Read, and outside the programme rule

* **Sundhed og informatik** (UCPH bachelor and master) - a health-informatics and digital-health degree; its stated subject is digital health rather than AI.
* **UCPH continuing education in AI and ML** - offered as individual courses and a self-assembled course package.
