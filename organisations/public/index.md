# Public organisations

Ministries, state agencies, KL, Danske Regioner, and municipalities. All entries are
**frame stubs** from pass 0.

* [KL](kl.md) - Local Government Denmark; interest organisation for the municipalities.
* [Danske Regioner](danske-regioner.md) - interest organisation for the five regions.

* [Digitaliseringsministeriet](digitaliseringsministeriet.md) - Ministry of Digital Affairs; secretariat for the Digital Taskforce and co-funder of CAISA.

*Expected to grow to include Digitaliseringsstyrelsen, Indenrigs- og Sundhedsministeriet,
and individual municipalities.*
