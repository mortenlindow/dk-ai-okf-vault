# Activities

| Programme | Level | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Machine learning og datavidenskab (BSc in Machine Learning and Data Science) | bachelor | UCPH's AI bachelor, study regulations from 2019 (rev. 2026). Belongs to the *Studienævn for matematik og datalogi*; gives right of admission to the MSc in Computer Science. |

A **row** rather than a `Programme`: its study regulations place it at a **single faculty and a
single study board** — *"Uddannelsen hører under Studienævn for matematik og datalogi"* (*"The programme belongs to the Study Board for Mathematics and Computer Science"*) — so
under the connectivity rule in `CLAUDE.md` §1 it is recorded here, on the owning faculty.
Its compulsory courses come from both computer science and mathematics — the regulations list
course codes from [DIKU](/organisations/academic/diku.md) and
[Mathematical Sciences](/organisations/academic/math-ku.md) — reflecting the joint study board.
The national listing is at [UddannelsesGuiden](/references/uddannelsesguiden.md).
