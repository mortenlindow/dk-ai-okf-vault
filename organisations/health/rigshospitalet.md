# AI presence

Rigshospitalet is identified in reporting and in publication affiliations as a site of
clinical AI work, including **CSTAR — Center for Surgical Translational and Artificial
Intelligence Research**, a research unit under its surgical departments.

CSTAR is recorded as a lead rather than a concept for now: under the No-News rule in
`CLAUDE.md` §1 a concept is written from the body's own durable page, and this vault has not
yet reached one for CSTAR. Possible further evolution: a standing CSTAR page, or a published
record on the Capital Region's research portal, would let it be written up in full.
