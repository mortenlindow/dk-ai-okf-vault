# Succession

**The single home of this succession.** As part of the health reform, Digital Sundhed
Danmark is established as a new national organisation that *"samler sundhed.dk,
Sundhedsdatastyrelsen, MedCom, Nationalt Genom Center og en række fælles nationale opgaver
fra regionerne"* (brings together sundhed.dk, the Danish Health Data Authority, MedCom, the
National Genome Centre and a number of shared national tasks from the regions) [1].

- **2026-01-01** — sundhed.dk, Sundhedsdatastyrelsen, MedCom and the regions' shared national
  IT solutions were gathered under joint management as *Det forberedende Digital Sundhed
  Danmark*, to prepare the transition [1].
- **2026-12-31** — sundhed.dk, Sundhedsdatastyrelsen and MedCom cease as independent
  organisations [1].
- **2027-01-01** — Digital Sundhed Danmark is established, owned jointly by municipalities,
  regions and the state, governed by a board appointed by the owners in the second half of
  2026, with offices in Aalborg, Odense and Copenhagen [1][2].

Selected functions and regulatory tasks of Sundhedsdatastyrelsen remain within the
ministry's group [1]. The page lists the National Genome Centre among the bodies brought
together; its list of organisations ceasing on 31 December 2026 names sundhed.dk,
Sundhedsdatastyrelsen and MedCom.
