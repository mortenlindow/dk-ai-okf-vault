# Succession

**The single home of this succession.** Under the Danish health reform, *"Region Sjælland
og Region Hovedstaden lægges sammen til Region Østdanmark. Den nye region er en realitet
1. januar 2027."* (*"Region Sjælland and Region Hovedstaden are merged into Region Østdanmark. The new region becomes a reality on 1 January 2027"*)

From **2027-01-01 Denmark has four regions** instead of five. Region Nordjylland, Region
Midtjylland and Region Syddanmark are unchanged. The three western regions' politicians took
office on 2026-01-01; the politicians of the two merging regions *"continue for one more year
and have operational responsibility for the two regions in their final year"* — so
Hovedstaden and Sjælland are **still operating** at this capture and are not yet dissolved.

The reform also establishes **17 new regional health councils (sundhedsråd)** to run health
services closer to citizens and hospitals. *These have not been researched; whether any
carries an AI remit is unknown.*

# Notes

**Consequences for this vault, not yet worked through.** Every AI body mapped here that sits
in Region Hovedstaden — [RAIT](/initiatives/rait.md) across four of its hospitals, and the
Capital Region hospital stubs — will belong to Region Østdanmark from 2027-01-01. Those
concepts keep their paths; their `Part of` rows will need updating **after** the date, not
before. A verification pass in January 2027 should sweep for this.

This is the **second** Danish public-health succession landing on 2027-01-01, alongside the
reported merger of [Sundhedsdatastyrelsen](/organisations/health/sundhedsdatastyrelsen.md),
sundhed.dk and MedCom. **The administrative layer beneath Danish health AI is being rebuilt
on a single date**, which makes early 2027 the most important scheduled verification point
in this vault.
