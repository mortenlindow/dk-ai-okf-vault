# Notes

Since 2026-01-01 the authority has been part of the preparatory Digital Sundhed Danmark, and
it ceases as an independent organisation on 2026-12-31; selected functions and regulatory
tasks remain within the ministry's group [2]. **The succession narrative has one home** —
the `# Succession` section of [Digital Sundhed Danmark](/organisations/health/digital-sundhed-danmark.md).
