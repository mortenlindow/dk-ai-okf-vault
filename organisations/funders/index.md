# Funders

Foundations, state research funds, Nordic and EU funders, and non-profit clusters.

The bodies here are funders; the money itself is modelled in [funding](../../funding/index.md).
Follow the money's purpose, not the donor's origin — a foundation's non-profit
grant-making is in scope even where the endowment came from a company.

* [Novo Nordisk Fonden](novo-nordisk-fonden.md) - Danish enterprise foundation and research funder.
* [Danmarks Grundforskningsfond](dnrf.md) - Danish National Research Foundation; funds Centres of Excellence.
* [Danish Life Science Cluster](danish-life-science-cluster.md) - non-profit national cluster for life science and welfare technology.

*Expected to grow to include Innovationsfonden, Danmarks Frie Forskningsfond, Villum
Fonden, Carlsbergfondet and Lundbeckfonden.*
