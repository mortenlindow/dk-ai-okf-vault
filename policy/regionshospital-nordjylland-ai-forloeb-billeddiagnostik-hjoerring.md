# Summary

A local guideline in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI for radiographers at the diagnostic-imaging department in Hjørring: the AI
fracture pathway, a five-day limit after trauma, flags and phrases, and staff's right to override
the AI [1].

# Key provisions

- Staff may always send the patient to the emergency department if they judge treatment necessary, whatever the AI's assessment.
- Sets a five-day limit after trauma for the AI pathway, with flags and documentation phrases.
