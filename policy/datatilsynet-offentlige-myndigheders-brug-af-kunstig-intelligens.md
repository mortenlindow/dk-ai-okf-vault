# Summary

Cross-sector guidance from the data-protection authority for public authorities about to develop
or use AI. It walks through the data-protection steps across the AI life cycle and uses regions,
hospitals and patient records among its examples [1].

# Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Form | guidance (vejledning), cross-sector |
| Domain | clinical |
| Issued by | Datatilsynet |
| Force | guidance |
| Date | October 2023 |
| Audience | project staff in public authorities and their data-protection advisers |
| Scope | Legal basis, purpose limitation and monitoring when public authorities develop and use AI |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Sets out the data-protection steps across the AI life cycle, from legal basis and purpose limitation to monitoring.
- A hospital may not start patient treatment on the basis of treatment suggestions a solution generates while it is still under development.
- The guidance states that it does not cover the AI Act, the Medical Device Regulation or the Health Act.
