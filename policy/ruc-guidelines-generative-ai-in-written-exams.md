Roskilde University's rules on generative AI in written exams, linked from its rectorate's hub page on

# Key provisions

- Generative AI is not allowed in written take-home, on-site or oral exams unless the activity description permits it.
- It is allowed in projects and theses with a description of use and quotation.
- Confidential, personal, non-anonymised or copyrighted material may not be shared with a generative-AI tool.
