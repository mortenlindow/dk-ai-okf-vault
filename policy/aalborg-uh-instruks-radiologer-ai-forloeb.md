# Summary

An instruction in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI for radiologists reporting on GP- or out-of-hours-referred patients in the AI
pathway: the workflow when the radiologist agrees or disagrees with the AI, and a conference track
for divergence between AI and the radiological report [1].

# Key provisions

- Sets the workflow for when the radiologist agrees and when they disagree with the AI.
- Divergence between AI and the radiological report goes to a dedicated conference track.
