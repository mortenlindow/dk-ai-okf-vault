# Summary

The University of Southern Denmark's exam rules on generative AI, adopted by its education council
and in force since September 2024 [1].

| Date | adopted 27 June 2024; in force 1 September 2024; page updated 18 December 2025 |
| Audience | students (the page addresses staff) |
| Scope | Generative AI at exams across the university |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Allowed in take-home assignments, bachelor projects and theses when disclosed; missing disclosure is treated as plagiarism.
- Not allowed at written on-site, other supervised and oral exams.
- Prompts may not contain personal or confidential data, including research secrets; exceptions are set in the course description.
