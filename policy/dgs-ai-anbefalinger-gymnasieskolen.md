# Summary

Nine recommendations from Danske Gymnasieelevers Sammenslutning, the upper-secondary pupils'
association, to the political and administrative level [1].

| Scope | AI in teaching, exams and cheating procedures in the gymnasium |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- A cross-subject course in digital literacy with a national curriculum and hours.
- A uniform national procedure when AI cheating is suspected, with presumption of innocence.
- New exam forms, including AI in oral-exam preparation and unprepared material in the exam room.
