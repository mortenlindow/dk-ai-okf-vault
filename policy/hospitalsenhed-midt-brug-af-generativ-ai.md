# Summary

A guideline in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok setting rules and frames for all staff of Hospitalsenhed Midt on
generative AI: which tool to use, which data may go where, and
staff's own responsibility for what they use [1].

# Key provisions

- Midtchat is the recommended starting point; it is not a closed system, so personal or confidential data, including CPR numbers, may not be entered.
- RMchat, the region's own tool, is the one tool staff may use for personal data and business-critical data.
- Copilot is recommended over other public tools.
- Generative AI may never alone form the basis of decisions on patient treatment; an answer becomes part of one's work only after one has assessed it.
