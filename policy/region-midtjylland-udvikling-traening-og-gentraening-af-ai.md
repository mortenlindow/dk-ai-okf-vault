# Summary

A regional guideline listing requirements for staff who develop, train or retrain AI tools in the
region: legal basis for patient data, IT security, and quality assurance under the Medical
Device Regulation [1].

# Key provisions

- As a rule, AI tools may not be developed, trained or retrained on live operation in patient treatment; static models are to be used in patient care.
- Patient data may be used on a legal basis such as consent, an approved research project or a quality project.
- If the algorithm is a medical device, the MDR's quality-assurance requirements must be met, with documentation.
