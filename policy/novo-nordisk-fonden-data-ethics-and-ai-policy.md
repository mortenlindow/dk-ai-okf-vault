# Summary

The foundation's data-ethics and AI policy: general principles for applicants, grantees and staff,
and specific rules for committee members and peer reviewers, modelled on the European Commission's
living guidelines [1].

# Key provisions

- Reviewers may not use AI to evaluate, score, compare or rank applications, and may not upload application content to an AI tool.
- Language help and general information look-ups are allowed when no application content goes in.
- AI tools are neither authors nor decision-makers in the foundation's evaluations, and the reviewer remains fully responsible.
- AI use in decision-making is to be disclosed.
