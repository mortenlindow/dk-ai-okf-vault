# Summary

A clinical instruction in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI, issued by the orthopaedic surgery department at Aalborg
University Hospital. It governs how doctors and nurse practitioners act on an AI fracture and
dislocation finding on limb X-rays; the text describes the AI as introduced as a decision-support
tool in all the region's emergency departments and injury clinics [1].

# Key provisions

- Staff may act on the AI result under the usual instructions, without a conference with a doctor, within the instruction's limits.
- Nurse practitioners may discharge patients in defined cases.
- The instruction states that AI fracture diagnostics, like all diagnostic methods, has false-positive and false-negative findings, and lists known cases.
- All X-rays from emergency departments and injury clinics are still reviewed at the daily X-ray conference.
