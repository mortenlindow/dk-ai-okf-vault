# Summary

The same ten recommendations as for the upper-secondary programmes, adapted for leaders and teachers
of the general subjects (*grundfag*) in vocational education [1].

| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- The same three themes and ten recommendations as for the upper-secondary programmes.
- AI may support learning even in subjects where it is not allowed at the final exam.

# Notes

The recommendations cover the general subjects; no specific programme, such as the social- and health-care programmes, is named.
