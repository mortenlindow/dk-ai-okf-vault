# Summary

An operational instruction in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI from the radiology department at Aalborg University Hospital:
which examinations are in or out of the AI pathway, which X-ray rooms send images automatically,
how to read the AI output and how to send images manually [1].

# Key provisions

- The AI provides decision support on selected X-ray images for suspected fracture or dislocation.
- Defines which examinations and rooms are included, and how images are sent to the AI manually.
