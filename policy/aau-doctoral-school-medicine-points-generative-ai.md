# Summary

Guidance from the doctoral school of Aalborg University's Faculty of Medicine, prepared with the
university library, applying the Danish code of conduct for research integrity to PhD students' use
of generative AI. Each point ends with a recommendation [1].

# Key provisions

- Using generative AI on one's own images or data may lead to suspicion of falsification or fabrication.
- AI platforms or language models can never be co-authors.
- Declare generative-AI use for language editing and for data collection or analysis in the methods section, and keep generated or synthetic data.
- Use only approved platforms with personal data, and keep manuscripts under review confidential.
