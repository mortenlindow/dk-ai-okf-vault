# Summary

The curriculum (*målbeskrivelse*) for specialist training in radiology, published by the health
authority and prepared by Dansk Radiologisk Selskab. Its foreword states that the rapid spread of AI
in radiology made a revision necessary, and it adds a competence on AI in radiology [1].

# Key provisions

- Competence 4.1: knowledge of the basic principles of artificial intelligence and their application in the organ-related areas, learned and assessed through approved specialty courses.
- Because AI develops quickly, examples are kept in a separate list on Dansk Radiologisk Selskab's site, updated as practice changes.
- The curriculum describes AI as becoming a major aid and decision support in radiology.
