# Summary

A regional guideline in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok, for staff who want to use
publicly available generative-AI tools. It sets out what may not be entered, the region's
responsibility as employer, and critical use of output [1].

# Key provisions

- Personal data, security-sensitive data and confidential data may not be used in — entered into or uploaded to — publicly available generative-AI tools.
- A decision must never be taken on the basis of output from a generative-AI solution alone.
- Output is to be used critically, and staff are to contact the region's information-security function with questions.
