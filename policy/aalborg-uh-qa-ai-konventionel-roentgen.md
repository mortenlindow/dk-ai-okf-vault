# Summary

An operational instruction in question-and-answer form in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI: inclusion criteria, opening hours,
flags and documentation phrases, what to do when the AI misses a finding, and who holds
responsibility for the patient pathway [1].

# Key provisions

- The orthopaedic surgery department is responsible for the patient pathway.
- Sets out documentation phrases and the procedure when the AI misses a finding.
