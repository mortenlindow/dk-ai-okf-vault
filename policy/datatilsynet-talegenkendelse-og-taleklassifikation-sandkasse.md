# Summary

The final report of a regulatory sandbox in which three municipalities and a supplier tested
generative AI that turns speech into text and summarises entries in health and care records. It
sets out the legal basis and the division of data-protection roles, and is published for other
authorities to use [1].

# Key provisions

- Examines the Health Act's provisions on processing for development and on records as the legal basis for an AI documentation solution.
- Finds that the project lacked a legal basis to develop the AI solution under the provision it first considered.
- A public authority's first step is to map the processing activities involved before assigning data-protection roles in a public–private partnership.
