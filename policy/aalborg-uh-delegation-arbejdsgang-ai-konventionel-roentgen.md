# Summary

A clinical guideline in Region Nordjylland's public document system, PRI whose body is a formal delegation of reserved clinical activity. It
authorises radiographers and X-ray nurses to send home patients referred by their GP or the
out-of-hours service when the AI finds no fracture [1].

# Key provisions

- A patient may be sent home without assessment in the emergency department if the X-ray AI does not show a fracture, within the delegation's conditions.
