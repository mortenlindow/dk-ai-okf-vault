# Summary

An instruction in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok for all staff in the pathology department who use AI in their work:
principles, pitfalls and data rules for the region's chat tools and external tools. Staff confirm
receipt [1].

# Key provisions

- AI is an aid, not a decision-maker.
- AI may not be used to make diagnoses or suggest treatment.
- Sets which data may be used in the region's chat tools and in external tools.
