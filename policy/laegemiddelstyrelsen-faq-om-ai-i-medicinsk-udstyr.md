# Summary

The medicines agency's FAQ on AI in medical devices: when AI-based software is classified as a
medical device, how it is classified under the EU Medical Device Regulation, and what CE marking
and clinical evidence require, including for self-learning systems [1].

# Key provisions

- AI-based software is classified as a medical device when it has a medical purpose, for example in diagnosis.
- Medical devices that use AI or machine learning must be CE-marked like any other medical device.
- The FAQ covers classification under the regulation's software rule, clinical evidence, and the particular questions raised by self-learning systems.

# Notes

The FAQ does not address the EU AI Act, which post-dates it, or hospitals' own in-house
production of AI devices, as read on 2026-11-02.
