# Summary

A shared specialty guideline in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok for general adult psychiatry across the region. A machine-
learning algorithm identifies each morning the patients at increased risk of mechanical restraint;
the guideline sets out how staff use the prediction, the preventive measures to consider, and
documentation [1].

# Key provisions

- The prediction can be used proactively; preventive measures are suggestions for staff to consider.
- Which patients the algorithm assesses as at increased risk must be documented daily.
- A patient leaflet on the prediction is available on request.
