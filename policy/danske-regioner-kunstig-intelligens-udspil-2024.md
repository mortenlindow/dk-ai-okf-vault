# Summary

The regions' joint policy proposal (*udspil*) on AI in the health service. It sets out principles
the regions have identified as guiding, and a procedure for agreeing national rollout of AI
solutions [1].

# Key provisions

- Eight principles are to guide the regions' direction on AI.
- One principle calls for new workflows and guidelines for clinicians using AI.
- The regions are to agree each year on national rollout of a number of AI solutions.
