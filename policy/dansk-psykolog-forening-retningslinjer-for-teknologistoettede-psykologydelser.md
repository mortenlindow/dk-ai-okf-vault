# Summary

A professional standard for psychologists on technology-supported services — consent, data
protection and record-keeping — with a distinct section on AI and automated tools, including
chat-based language models [1].

# Key provisions

- AI and automated tools should not replace the psychologist's professional assessment, responsibility or relational work.
- Where chat-based language models are used, it should be clear that the technology is not therapy.
- Psychologists should assess AI tools before use and inform clients; they remain bound by the profession's ethical principles.
