# Summary

The education agency's list of permitted aids at the upper-secondary exams. With the subject curricula
and the exam order, it forms the authoritative set of rules, and it overrides guidance pages [1].

| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- AI functions built into permitted tools are prohibited as a starting point.
- For exams prepared away from school, all aids are allowed except generative AI.
- Where a subject's own exam allows generative AI, it may be used for that subject's part only; the hf exam project may take generative AI as its topic, with chatbot output marked and cited.
