# Summary

The business school's exam rules on generative AI, with sections for each role in the exam [1].

# Facts

| | |
|---|---|

| Domain | education |
| Level | higher education; continuing professional education |
| Issued by | Copenhagen Business School (Educational Development and Quality) |
| Force | binding |
| Date | in force 1 September 2026; revised July 2026 |
| Audience | students, teachers, supervisors, assessors and examiners |
| Scope | Generative AI at written and oral exams and in final projects |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Generative AI is allowed by default in written exams and final projects, except restricted on-site exams; course-level bans cannot apply to degree-concluding projects.
- Proofreading and search need no declaration; idea generation goes in the methods section; generated content needs a reference.
- No generative-AI answers during oral exams; students use the school's GDPR-compliant tools.
