# Summary

An instruction in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok for a quality project in which a CE-marked AI interprets thrombolysis MR
scans in stroke: blinded reading, documentation, and the population it applies to [1].

# Key provisions

- A radiologist who departs from the AI interpretation must document it clearly in the report.
- Sets blinded-reading, documentation and population limits for the project.

# Notes

The text states that the project runs until 1 July 2026; the document is still listed as in force as of 2026-11-02. The two dates are reported side by side.
