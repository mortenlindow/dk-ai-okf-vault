# Summary

The agency's page interpreting the folkeskole exam order for generative AI, covering every school form
that holds the folkeskole's exams, with one recommendation of its own [1].

| Scope | Generative AI at written, oral and product-based folkeskole exams |

| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- No text-generating AI at any written exam taken at school, and none on exam day at oral exams, including preparation time.
- For exam products made during teaching, the school board decides the permitted aids; the agency recommends not allowing generative AI.
- Passing off a chatbot's work as one's own counts as cheating.

# Notes

The exam order itself (BEK nr. 678 of 2025) does not name AI; the rules sit in its interpretation on this page.
