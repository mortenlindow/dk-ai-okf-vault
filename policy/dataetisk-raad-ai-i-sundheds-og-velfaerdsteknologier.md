# Summary

A report from the Data Ethics Council, the first part of a two-part project, built on practice
experience, professional dialogues and a symposium. It identifies nine findings and six
data-ethical principles and makes four recommendations for responsible use of AI in health and
welfare technologies [2].

# Key provisions

- AI should be used in the health and welfare sector only if the solution can be expected to bring real benefit to patients, citizens or professionals.
- AI solutions should be developed and implemented with the involvement of all relevant actors.
- AI solutions should be used only if they can be — and are — explained in a way that is meaningful to those affected.
- Common frameworks for knowledge, collaboration and support should be established, so that advice on AI is easy to obtain.

# Notes

The recommendations were read on the council's own page announcing the report [2]; the report is
the resource. On 29 June 2026 the council launched a free online workshop, *AI i sundheds- og
velfærdsteknologier*, built on three of the four recommendations [3].
