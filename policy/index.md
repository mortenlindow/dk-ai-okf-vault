# Policy

Normative instruments that bind or steer in-scope bodies: national strategies, statutes,
tripartite agreements, rules of procedure.

The *issuing body* is an [organisation](../organisations/index.md); the *document* is a
concept here; the link is an `Issued by` row. Never merge them.

Press releases, news items and position papers with no addressee are **citations**, not
concepts.
