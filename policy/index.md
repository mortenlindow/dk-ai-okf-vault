# Policy

Normative instruments that bind or steer in-scope bodies — statutes, executive orders,
guidance, local instructions, rules of procedure — and institutional input on them.

The *issuing body* is an [organisation](../organisations/index.md) where it has a file; the
*document* is a concept here; the link is an `Issued by` row. Never merge them.

This folder holds **the rules registers** on AI: clinical use, read in
[Rules and guidance on clinical AI](../views/clinical-ai-rules/index.md), and research and
education, read in [Rules and guidance on AI in research and education](../views/research-education-ai-rules/index.md).
One file per document; `Domain` says which use of AI it governs and `Force` whether it is binding,
guidance, a recommendation or input. Grouped below by the level of the issuer, with the domain in
brackets.
