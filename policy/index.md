# Policy

Normative instruments that bind or steer in-scope bodies — statutes, executive orders,
guidance, local clinical instructions, rules of procedure — and institutional input on them.

The *issuing body* is an [organisation](../organisations/index.md) where it has a file; the
*document* is a concept here; the link is an `Issued by` row. Never merge them.

This folder holds **the clinical-AI rules register**, read as a whole in
[Rules and guidance on clinical AI](../views/clinical-ai-rules/index.md). One file per document;
`Force` records whether it is binding, guidance, a recommendation or input.

## National — statute and authorities

* [Lov om supplerende bestemmelser til forordningen om kunstig intelligens (lov nr. 467 af 14. maj 2025)](lov-om-supplerende-bestemmelser-til-forordningen-om-kunstig-intelligens.md) - *binding*. * [Ansvar ved brug af kunstig intelligens AI (Digital sundhedsfaglig behandling: Ansvar og pligter)](stps-ansvar-ved-brug-af-kunstig-intelligens.md) - *guidance*. Styrelsen for Patientsikkerhed's guidance on professional responsibility when AI is used in treatment.

* [Offentlige myndigheders brug af kunstig intelligens – Inden I går i gang (Datatilsynet)](datatilsynet-offentlige-myndigheders-brug-af-kunstig-intelligens.md) - *guidance*. Datatilsynet's data-protection guidance for public authorities starting AI projects, with examples from hospitals and patient records.
* [Talegenkendelse og taleklassifikation som støtteværktøj for personale inden for sundheds- og omsorgsområdet (slutrapport, sandkasseforløb)](datatilsynet-talegenkendelse-og-taleklassifikation-sandkasse.md) - *guidance*. Final report of Datatilsynet's and Digitaliseringsstyrelsen's regulatory sandbox on generative-AI speech recognition for health and care records.
* [Guide til offentlige myndigheder om brug af (generativ) kunstig intelligens (Digitaliseringsstyrelsen)](digitaliseringsstyrelsen-guide-om-brug-af-generativ-kunstig-intelligens.md) - *guidance*. Digitaliseringsstyrelsen's guide for leaders of public authorities on choosing an approach to generative-AI tools and writing internal rules.
* [AI i sundheds- og velfærdsteknologier — fire anbefalinger (Dataetisk Råd)](dataetisk-raad-ai-i-sundheds-og-velfaerdsteknologier.md) - *recommendation*. Dataetisk Råd's four recommendations for responsible, data-ethical use of AI in the health and welfare sector.
