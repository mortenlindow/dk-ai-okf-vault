# Summary

The patients' umbrella organisation's response to the draft amendment of the elder-care act on
developing and using AI and similar digital solutions in municipal elder care, including the use
of health data. It supports the development and sets out conditions [2].

# Key provisions

- AI solutions may be used only as decision support; decisions must always be taken by a competent employee with a duty to make an independent professional assessment — and this should be written into the rules.
- Health data and other confidential information should be used only for purposes within the elder-care act, and not for commercial or control purposes beyond what the act requires.
- Citizens should be told in advance that their health information can be used for AI solutions.

# Notes

Elder care sits at the edge of this register's health scope; the response is included because it
concerns health data and decision support. The PDF was not opened; the points above are from the
organisation's own page summarising it [2].
