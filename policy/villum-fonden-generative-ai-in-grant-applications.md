# Summary

A white paper, written by two University of Cambridge researchers for the foundation, presenting a
draft policy on generative AI in grant applications co-created at a workshop with about 100 of the
foundation's grantees. It is offered as a starting point for foundations writing their own
policies [1].

# Key provisions

- Each applicant remains fully responsible for the content and must check all content and references.
- AI is encouraged for language and flow, and allowed for brainstorming and exploring literature already identified.
- Applicants should disclose which tools they used and for what; application forms could carry checkboxes for this.
