# Summary

A page maintained by [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md) for staff of Odense University Hospital, Region
Syddanmark and SDU, setting out what the region's own chat platform, SydChat, may and may not be
used for, which data may not be entered, and the mandatory course for staff who use generative
AI [1].

# Key provisions

- SydChat may be used for writing, summarising and planning support, but not for clinical decisions or patient assessments.
- CPR numbers, health information and other sensitive or confidential data may not be entered.
- Staff who use generative AI in their work must complete the region's e-learning course.
