# Summary

An advisory guide from [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md) for people running AI projects, written with Region
Syddanmark in mind: data-protection steps, whom to contact in the region, ongoing validation, and
what to settle before going live [1].

# Key provisions

- Carry out a data-protection impact assessment, and a data-processing agreement where a company is involved.
- Plan ongoing validation of the AI solution after it is in use.
- Settle questions such as CE marking and automated decisions before going live.
