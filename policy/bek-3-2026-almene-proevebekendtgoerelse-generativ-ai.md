# Summary

The executive order governing exams in the upper-secondary programmes and general adult education.
Its clause on aids names generative AI explicitly [1].

| Force | binding |

| Audience | institutions and examinees |
| Scope | Aids at upper-secondary exams, including generative AI |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Digital tools, including generative AI, may not be used unless the rules for the exam or the official list of aids allow it.
- Special exam conditions may allow extra tools, but never generative AI.
- The education agency publishes the list of permitted aids.
