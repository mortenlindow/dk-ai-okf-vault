# Summary

A supplement turning the seven steps of the legal guidance into a practical checklist, with cases such
as AI feedback on assignments, AI cheating detection and facial recognition for attendance [1].

| Audience | teachers, school leaders and rectors |
| Scope | Practical steps before an education institution takes an AI solution into use |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- The institution carries the responsibility, and leadership should decide on adopting an AI solution.
- Schools should set an AI policy and a list of approved tools.
