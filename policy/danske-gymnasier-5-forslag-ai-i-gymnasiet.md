# Summary

Five proposals from Danske Gymnasier, the association of upper-secondary schools and their leaders, for
AI in teaching and exams [1].

# Key provisions

- A trial with AI in the final major research assignment (SRP), and trials of process-based exam forms.
- Access to a trusted encyclopaedia at all written exams.
- A mandatory digital-technology subject, and funded continuing education for teachers.
