# Summary

Cross-sector guidance for leaders of public authorities, regions among them, on generative-AI
tools: choosing an approach, writing internal guidelines for staff, handling personal data and
confidentiality, and the risk of incorrect output. The health service is not named [1].

# Key provisions

- Personal data must not be entered into generative-AI tools that are freely available on public websites.
- Each authority is responsible for making sure its use of generative AI is responsible, and is advised to write internal guidelines for staff.
- Addresses the risk that generative AI produces incorrect content (hallucination).
