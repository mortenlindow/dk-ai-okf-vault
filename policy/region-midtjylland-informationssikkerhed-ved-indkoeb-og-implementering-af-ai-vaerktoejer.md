# Summary

A regional guideline that sets a procedure for staff who buy or implement AI tools, from first
contact with the information-security function to the conditions for tools used in patient
treatment [1].

# Key provisions

- The region's information-security function is to be contacted first.
- AI tools used in patient treatment must run as static models in operation.
- CE-marked tools are recommended and must be used according to the manufacturer's instructions; data-protection impact assessments, data-processing agreements and cloud safeguards apply.
- Medical devices with AI follow the regional instruction for buying medical-technical equipment.
