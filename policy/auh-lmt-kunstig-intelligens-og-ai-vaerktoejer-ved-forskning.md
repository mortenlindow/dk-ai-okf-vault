# Summary

A short note in Region Midtjylland's public document system, filed as an instruction by the
hepatology and gastroenterology department at Aarhus University Hospital, cautioning on AI in health
research and pointing to reading. It calls itself a living document [1].

- Points to the Faculty of Health's network on AI in health research and to further reading.
