# Summary

The student portal's rules on generative AI at Aarhus University, following a decision by the
university's Education Committee effective from autumn 2024 [1].

# Key provisions

- Generative AI is allowed unless the academic regulations or course catalogue prohibit it; where prohibited, even proofreading by it is banned.
- Use is declared on the university's template; no declaration is needed at on-site exams.
- Generative AI may not do the exam project for the student, and sensitive personal data may never be uploaded.

# Notes

The Faculty of Health exempted the bachelor project and master's thesis in medicine and dentistry
from the university's 2024 easing for a period; that decision is known from faculty news items and is
not filed.
