# Summary

A position paper from FYAM, the committee for young general practitioners within DSAM, setting
out eight positions on the conditions for using AI in general practice [1].

# Key provisions

- AI should give the doctor a clinical option, not take the decision.
- AI must never be used as cover (*rygdækning*) in clinical assessments.
- General practitioners should be involved in decisions on AI; administrative tasks and research come first; clear rules on when AI may not be used, and an exit strategy, are recommended.
