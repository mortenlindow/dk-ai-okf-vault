# Summary

The faculty's declaration form, putting SDU's GAI rules into practice. It is mandatory as an annex to
the medicine thesis; for other programmes its use varies [1].

| Access | open |

# Key provisions

- The form must be attached to the medicine thesis.
- The student states whether GAI was used, which tools and versions, the input, and how the output was used.
- Missing disclosure is treated as plagiarism; sensitive personal data may not be entered.
