# Summary

The Danish act that supplements the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). It names the national
competent authorities and their powers. It applies across sectors, the health service included,
and contains no health-specific provision [1].

# Key provisions

- Digitaliseringsstyrelsen, Datatilsynet and Domstolsstyrelsen are the national competent authorities under the AI Act.
- Decisions of the national competent authorities cannot be appealed to another administrative authority.

# Notes

A bill under the same title with the short name *AI-loven* (2026/1 LSF 3) was tabled on
7 October 2026 [2]. Under it the minister would designate the authorities by order; it contains no
health-specific provision either. Until it is passed, the 2025 act is recorded as the instrument in
force; the bill is noted here side by side.
