# Summary

The University of Southern Denmark's overarching principles for AI, reviewed yearly; detailed research
rules are planned as separate frameworks [1].

# Key provisions

- Any use of AI in academic, administrative and research contexts must be transparent, following the norms of the field.
- The person presenting AI output must check it critically.
- Personal data may not be shared with AI systems without a data-processing agreement or compliance assessment.
