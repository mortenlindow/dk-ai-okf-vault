# Summary

Seven recommendations for school leaders, teachers, school boards and municipalities on generative AI in
teaching. The document notes that there are no national guidelines for AI in folkeskole teaching [1].

| Access | open |

# Key provisions

- Leadership and the school board set a shared direction and framework.
- Use GDPR-compliant solutions; the municipality is responsible for compliance.
- Build staff competence and use AI only where it makes subject, didactic and pedagogical sense.
- Talk with pupils about AI use in each subject, inform parents, and make sure pupils can sit the final exams without generative AI.
