# Summary

The national education order for the specialist education in acute-care nursing, written by its
national education council and approved by the health authority, sets rules for AI in home
assignments, the synopsis exam and the final exam. It is published locally in Region Midtjylland's
document system by Regionshospitalet Gødstrup's emergency department [1].

# Key provisions

- Participants may use AI in home assignments and in written and oral exams when the guidelines are followed.
- Use must be stated, for example in the method section, with at least the tool and how it was used.
- AI-generated text that is used is quoted like any other quotation; participants are expected to reflect on their choice of tools.
