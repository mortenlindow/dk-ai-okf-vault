# Summary

Aalborg University's general rules on generative AI for students, set by its strategic council for
education and implemented by the study boards, with an accompanying FAQ [1][2].

# Key provisions

- Generative AI may be used like other aids unless the curriculum or module description says otherwise.
- Students are to be open about use and cite AI material; AI must never write an entire assignment.
- Keeping records of prompts, outputs and model versions is recommended; students are not required to use generative AI.
