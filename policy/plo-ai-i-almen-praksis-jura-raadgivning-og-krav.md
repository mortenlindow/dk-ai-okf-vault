# Summary

Guidance from the general practitioners' organisation for clinics considering AI tools for
notes, referrals, certificates and patient summaries. It addresses the practice as data
controller and sets out the legal steps before use [1].

# Key provisions

- A practice needs a valid legal basis, and a data-processing agreement with the supplier is not necessarily a sufficient basis.
- A data-protection impact assessment and risk assessment are recommended before use; PLO considers AI use in general practice likely to be high-risk.
- The practice must assess the need for, and degree of, patient information and consent.
- Legal advice and attention to bias are part of the preparation.
