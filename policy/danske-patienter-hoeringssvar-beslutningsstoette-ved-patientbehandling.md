# Summary

The patients' umbrella organisation's response to the Ministry of the Interior and Health's draft
executive order on using personal data for decision support in patient treatment, which would
set the frame for developing and running electronic decision-support tools, AI-based solutions
included. It welcomes the potential and sets out conditions [1].

# Key provisions

- Decisions on examination, diagnosis and treatment must always be taken by an authorised health professional on a concrete, individual assessment; AI is support, not a replacement.
- The order could state still more clearly that AI systems may not be used alone for automated decisions on patient treatment.
- Patients and relatives should be involved in developing and using AI-based decision-support tools.
- Responsible, publicly anchored or clearly regulated patient-facing AI solutions should be developed, with non-digital alternatives kept for those who need them.
