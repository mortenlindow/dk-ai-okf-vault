# Summary

Rules on data, security and copyright when staff and students use generative AI at SDU, with
sections for researchers and teachers. The page states it is still being implemented [1].

# Key provisions

- Never use personal data, including sensitive or confidential personal data, as input unless the tool is approved for it; tools have three approval levels.
- Researchers may use local systems for research projects under their own and management's responsibility.
- Students cannot be required to create accounts on tools that are not centrally approved.
