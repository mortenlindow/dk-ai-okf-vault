# Summary

The draft bill that gives effect to the political agreement of 24 September 2026 on the hf major
written assignment (SSO). It turns the assignment into one exam with a written product and an oral
examination, for two-year hf and for hf-enkeltfag. The general remarks give AI as the reason: no
exam grade should rest on home-written work alone [1][2].

# Key provisions

- The SSO on two-year hf and on hf-enkeltfag becomes one exam: the written assignment forms the basis for an oral examination.
- The academic aims and content of the written product are unchanged; the curriculum is to be adjusted for the oral part.
- Proposed entry into force 1 January 2027, for students who begin the writing period after that date.
- Institutions are compensated through the state taximeter grants; the remarks put the cost at DKK 5.0m a year 2027–2031.
