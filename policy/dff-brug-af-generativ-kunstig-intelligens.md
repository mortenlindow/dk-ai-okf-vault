# Summary

The Independent Research Fund Denmark's rules on generative AI in applications and in its own
assessment of them [1][2].

# Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Form | rules (grant conditions) |
| Domain | research |
| Issued by | Danmarks Frie Forskningsfond (DFF) |
| Force | binding for applicants (disclosure) and for assessment members |
| Date | not stated on the pages (checked 2026-11-02) |
| Audience | applicants; members of the fund's council and committees |
| Scope | AI in writing applications and in assessing them |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Applicants must state in the application form's AI section whether they used generative AI to a significant extent; assessors do not see the answer.
- Use is permitted when responsible, and the applicant remains responsible for the output.
- The fund does not use AI to assess applications, and members may not upload applications to AI systems.
