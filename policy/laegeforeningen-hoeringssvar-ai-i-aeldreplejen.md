# Summary

The Danish Medical Association's response to the draft amendment of the elder-care act on
developing and using AI in municipal elder care. It comments on the minister's powers, secondary
use of health data, profiling, and independent professional judgement [1].

# Key provisions

- AI should support, not steer, professional assessments and decisions.
- The minister's powers under the bill, the secondary use of health data and profiling of citizens should be limited.
