# Key provisions

- AI may help prepare and process incoming applications.
- Grant decisions are made by people, not by AI.
- AI output is never used without human oversight and validation, and substantial AI-generated material is disclosed.
- AI is not used where it could conflict with applicants' rights, or in recruitment.
