# Summary

The health faculty's teaching page sets its own limits on AI in the medicine thesis, which are stricter
than SDU's general rules: AI as support, not as author [1].

# Key provisions

- AI may be used as support and sparring partner for preparation, structure, proofreading and feedback.
- AI may not be used to generate text or answers.
- SDU's general AI rules must be followed, and students declare where and for what AI was used, using the faculty's template.
