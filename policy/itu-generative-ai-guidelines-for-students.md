The IT University's student guidelines on generative AI, in their fourth version [1].

# Key provisions

- The exam rule is set per course — allowed, restricted or not allowed — in the course description.
- Generative AI is always allowed in projects, including theses, when the documentation requirements are met.
- State the tool and how it was used, including in code; feeding copyrighted material to chatbots without consent is not allowed.
