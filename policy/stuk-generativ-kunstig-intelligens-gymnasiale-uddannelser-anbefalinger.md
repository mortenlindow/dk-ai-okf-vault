# Summary

Ten recommendations in three themes — a leadership framework, teacher competence, and teaching in, with
and without AI — for daily teaching and written work. Exams are left to the expert group [1].

# Key provisions

- Leadership sets a shared direction and concrete rules for teachers' and pupils' use.
- Use solutions that leadership judges GDPR-compliant, and develop teacher competence.
- Pupils are to be taught in, with and without generative AI; written work is planned so pupils learn to use AI to improve their process.
