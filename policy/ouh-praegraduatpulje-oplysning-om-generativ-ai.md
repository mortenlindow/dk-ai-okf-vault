# Summary

The call for the hospital's internal fund for pre-graduate research stipends requires applicants
to disclose generative-AI use. Of the hospital's 27 internal-fund pages checked, it is the one with
such a clause [1].

# Key provisions

- If generative AI was used, the application must state for which parts, and which AI assistant and version.
