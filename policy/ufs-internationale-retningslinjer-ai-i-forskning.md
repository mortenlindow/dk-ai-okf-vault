# Summary

A page kept by the agency that lists the international guidelines on AI in research to which the
2026 Danish code of conduct refers. It sets no Danish rules of its own and is updated as
guidelines change [1].

# Key provisions

- Lists the ALLEA European Code of Conduct (2023 revision), the European Commission's living guidelines on responsible use of generative AI in research (2025), and UNESCO's guidance for generative AI in education and research (2023).
- States that the overview will be updated as guidelines change.
