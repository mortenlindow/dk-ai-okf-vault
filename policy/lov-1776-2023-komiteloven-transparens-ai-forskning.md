# Summary

An amendment to the act on ethics review of health research and health-data-science projects. Its new
provision requires disclosure of researchers' fees and financial ties when a health-data-science
project using imaging data from patient records has a commercial sponsor. The bill's remarks present
it as transparency about financial interests in AI research, meaning decision-support systems trained
on such imaging data; the enacted text itself does not use the words *kunstig intelligens* [1][2].

# Key provisions

- When a health-data-science project uses imaging data from patient records and has a commercial sponsor, the committee must be told the researcher's fee and the relevant contract clauses.
- Other financial ties between researcher and sponsor must also be disclosed.
- The committee must ensure the researcher is not unduly influenced.
