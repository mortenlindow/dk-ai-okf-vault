# Summary

Guidance for the trade committees that set each vocational programme's training order, on whether and
how AI may form part of the final exam and other programme-specific exams. It applies to every
programme's committee, so the social- and health-care programmes' committee is covered by
implication [1].

# Key provisions

- AI should be used in exams only where it is needed to test the exam's goals.
- AI should not be allowed as an aid where the competence must be mastered without it.
- The exam must leave no doubt that the work is the examinee's own; example wording shows how AI can enter the exam's goals, basis and criteria.
