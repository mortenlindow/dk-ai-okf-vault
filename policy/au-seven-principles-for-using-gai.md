# Summary

Seven principles for staff use of generative AI across Aarhus University, drawn up by a working group
including the research administration; the Graduate School of Health cites them for PhD
students [1].

# Key provisions

- Generative AI may not be used for anything involving trade secrets, confidential or sensitive data or copyrighted material.
- The user is responsible for the quality of the output, and generative-AI use is credited.
- Generative AI is not a source that can be cited as an authority; purchases of tools are made centrally.
