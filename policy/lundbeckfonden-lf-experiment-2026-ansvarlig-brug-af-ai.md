# Summary

An item in the applicant guide for one call, requiring applicants to describe how AI was used in
preparing their application. No foundation-wide AI policy or reviewer rule was found [1].

# Key provisions

- Applicants who used AI must describe how — for conception, writing, summaries, grammar, figures and the like — and which tools.
