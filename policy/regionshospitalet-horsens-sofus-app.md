# Summary

A local guideline in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok on an app that monitors admitted patients' electronic record data with
the help of AI and notifies staff of risk of sepsis and organ failure: how to acknowledge, act on
and document the notifications [1].

# Key provisions

- The app's notifications cannot be used as an independent basis for patient treatment.
- Sets out how notifications are acknowledged, acted on and documented.
