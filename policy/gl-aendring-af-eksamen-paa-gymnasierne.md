# Summary

Proposals from Gymnasielærerne, the upper-secondary teachers' union, adding to the minister's
package against AI cheating [1][2].

# Key provisions

- Mandatory, state-certified monitoring at exams.
- A one-hour analog part in written exams, shorter oral presentations and unprepared material at oral exams.
- Declared AI use allowed for information search and writing guidance in the major written assignments; a national standard limiting AI in commenting on or grading pupils' work.
