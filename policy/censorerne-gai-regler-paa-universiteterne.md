# Summary

Guidance from the secretariat of the external-examiner corps for business, social-science and
communication subjects, telling examiners how to handle generative AI given that each university
sets its own rules. It summarises and links the rules of six universities [1].

# Key provisions

- Examiners must find out the GAI rules of the university where they examine.
- An examiner who suspects GAI cheating tells the internal examiner and follows the usual cheating procedure.
- Summarises and links the rules of KU, RUC, AAU, AU, CBS and SDU.

# Notes

This corps does not cover the health sciences; the medical examiners' corps's letter to the universities on language models is a lead not yet read.
