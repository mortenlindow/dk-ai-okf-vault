# Summary

A policy paper from the Danish Medical Association with ten proposals for using digitalisation to
relieve doctors. AI appears in a box on artificial intelligence and in one proposal on collecting
and processing patient data [1].

# Facts

|---|---|

| Force | input — not authoritative (policy proposal addressed to regions and authorities) |

| Scope | Digitalisation in the health service; one proposal on AI for collecting and processing patient data |
| Access | open |
| Status | Unknown |

# Key provisions

- The regions, together with RKKP (the clinical quality databases), should assess how artificial intelligence can be taken into use.

# Notes

AI is one element among ten proposals; the paper is recorded because its AI proposal is distinct.
