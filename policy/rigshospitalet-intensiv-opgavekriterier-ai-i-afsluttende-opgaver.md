# Summary

Assignment criteria for the final written assignment of the specialist education in intensive-care
nursing, published in Region Hovedstaden's document system by Rigshospitalet's intensive-care unit,
with a section and an annex on AI. The annex, from the education's chair of examiners, calls itself a
first, provisional version [1].

# Key provisions

- AI tools can help, but they cannot be co-authors; generative AI is cited with author, year, title, type and publisher.
- Use is described in the method section, with prompts and outputs in an annex.
- AI may be used for idea generation, initial literature search, grammar and editing, but not to produce finished text for substantial sections.
- Suspected improper use of AI is handled under the rules on cheating.
