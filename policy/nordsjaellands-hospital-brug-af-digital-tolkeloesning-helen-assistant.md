# Summary

A guideline in Region Hovedstaden's public document system, VIP, approved by the hospital's
executive board, on the regional AI interpreter app: when it may and may not be used in dialogue
with patients and relatives, and the duty to document [1].

# Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Form | guideline (vejledning) |
| Issued by | Nordsjællands Hospital (approved by its executive board), [Region Hovedstaden](/organisations/health/region-hovedstaden.md) |
| Force | binding; management ensures staff know it and each employee must follow it |
| Date | in force from 5 September 2026 |
| Updated | version 1; next revision 4 September 2029 |
| Audience | staff with patient contact |
| Scope | Permitted use of the regional AI interpreter app, and a ban on other translation tools |
| Access | open |
| Status | Active |

# Key provisions

- Describes the use of the regional AI interpreter application at the region's hospitals.
- Translation tools other than the regional app may not be used in dialogue with patients and relatives.
- Use of the app is to be documented.

# Notes

Bispebjerg and Frederiksberg Hospital (from 9 July 2026) and Herlev and Gentofte Hospital (from
25 August 2026) have parallel guidelines on the same app; their texts do not call it AI, so they
are listed in the register view rather than filed. Herlev and Gentofte's states that a regional
document will replace it. Region Sjælland's guideline on the same app is
[recorded](/policy/region-sjaelland-tolkning-helen-assistant.md).
