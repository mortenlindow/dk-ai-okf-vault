# Summary

The report of the expert group appointed by the Minister for Children and Education in May 2023, with
four recommendations on exams. Vocational and preparatory education are treated more briefly, as
their exams are set locally [1].

# Key provisions

- Exams should stand on two legs, testing both with and without digital aids, within seven generic exam forms.
- Exam forms should be changeable faster, and teaching should form the basis for new exam forms.
- Digital technology and AI should be a strategic priority; the report notes there are no tools that can determine whether AI has been used.
