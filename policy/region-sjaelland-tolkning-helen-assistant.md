# Summary

Region Sjælland's guideline on ordering and conducting interpreting, with a section on the AI
interpreter app Helen Assistant: when it may be used, and when a professional interpreter is
required [1].

# Facts

| Issued by | [Region Sjælland](/organisations/health/region-sjaelland.md); the app rules adopted by the steering group of its *AI Tolkeløsning* project, April and June 2026 |
| Force | employees must know and follow it |

| Audience | staff of Region Sjælland |
| Scope | Use of the AI interpreter app as a supplement, and when professional interpreters are required |
| Access | open |

# Key provisions

- The AI interpreter app is a supplement for short practical communication.
- Professional interpreters are required for treatment conversations.
- The app was to be available from 31 August 2026.

# Notes

Hospitals in Region Hovedstaden have their own guidelines on the same app; see
[Nordsjællands Hospital](/policy/nordsjaellands-hospital-brug-af-digital-tolkeloesning-helen-assistant.md).
