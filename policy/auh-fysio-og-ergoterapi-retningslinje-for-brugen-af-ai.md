# Summary

A department guideline in Region Midtjylland's public document system, e-Dok on language models for staff in physiotherapy and occupational
therapy: which tools may be used and for what [1].

# Key provisions

- Copilot and the region's own chat tool are the tools permitted.
- AI may not be used for clinical decision support.
- The region's chat tool may be used, for example, to summarise text copied manually from patient records.
