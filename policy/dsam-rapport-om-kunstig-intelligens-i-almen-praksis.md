# Summary

The Danish College of General Practitioners' report on what AI and advanced health technology
could do in general practice, the pitfalls, and its overall recommendations for implementation.
Annexes relate the technology to DSAM's guiding principles, list tools wished for in general
practice, and set out the legal framework [2].

# Key provisions

- AI should be a decision-support tool, not a decision-making tool.
- Patients should know whether a treatment proposal comes from the doctor or from AI.
- Patient safety requires evidence and research before AI is used in practice; the report also addresses data use, responsibility and legislation.
