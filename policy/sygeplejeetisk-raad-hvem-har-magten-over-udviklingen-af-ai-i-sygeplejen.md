# Summary

A statement from the Nordic network of nurses' ethics councils, signed for Denmark by the chair
of Sygeplejeetisk Råd under Dansk Sygeplejeråd, on who holds power over the development of AI in
nursing and the conditions for its use [1].

# Key provisions

- AI should support nursing, not replace human assessment.
- Nurses must be trained in AI and take part in the decisions on its development and use.
- Ethical analyses should be carried out when AI is introduced in care.
