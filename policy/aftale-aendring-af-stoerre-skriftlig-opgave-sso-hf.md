# Summary

A political agreement among the parties to the upper-secondary settlement to add an oral exam to the hf
major written assignment, so that no exam grade rests solely on written work done at home. A bill is
to follow [1].

# Key provisions

- An oral exam is added to the major written assignment from school year 2026/27.
- Schools receive DKK 5m a year 2027–2031 in compensation.
- A coming expert group for the other upper-secondary programmes is to address the role of written work in light of AI.

# Notes

The agreement follows a ministerial package against AI cheating announced on 6 August 2026 (oral
defence of home-written papers, exam monitoring tools, more written work at school), known from the
ministry's news items and not filed. The agreement and the package both refer to a coming national AI
strategy for the school and education sector.
