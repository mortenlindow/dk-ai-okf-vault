AI in Danish Health

Public and non-profit AI-in-health activity in Denmark is organised at three levels: networks that reach across the country, centres and networks inside university faculties, and bodies anchored at hospitals and regions. Where founding dates are recorded they run from 2019 to 2026. The earliest are hospital-anchored; the university networks date from 2026. On 1 January 2027 the regional and national health administration around them is reorganised.

Netværk for AI i Sundhed

A politically independent, cross-disciplinary network, founded in 2025, working for AI in Danish healthcare that is developed and implemented on the basis of clinical needs. It convenes clinicians, researchers, technology developers and decision-makers across hospitals, universities, companies and public authorities, and works from two sides: systematic needs assessment, with a focus on health equity, and competence building in the health service and among developers alike.

How it is run

In its start-up phase the network is run by a coordination group with representatives from hospitals, universities, private companies and public authorities. Working groups are open: a member may join one or more.

A new administrative setting

Under the health reform, the regional and national administration around these bodies changes on a single date. The bodies themselves keep their identity; what changes is where they sit.

ChangeWhat happensBodies on this page
Region Østdanmark1 Jan 2027
What happens

Region Hovedstaden and Region Sjælland merge, and Denmark has four regions instead of five. Their politicians have operational responsibility for the two regions through 2026.

Bodies on this page

RAIT and the AI-Innovationsnetværket are recorded in Region Hovedstaden and will sit in Region Østdanmark.

Digital Sundhed Danmark1 Jan 2027
What happens

A national organisation, jointly owned by municipalities, regions and the state, brings together sundhed.dk, Sundhedsdatastyrelsen, MedCom, the National Genome Centre and shared national tasks from the regions. A preparatory organisation has run since 1 January 2026; the owners appoint a board in the second half of 2026.

Bodies on this page

Its stated tasks include better use of health data across the health service. An AI remit is not yet recorded in the vault.

Looking ahead

Possible further evolution

In the landscape

  • Two open networks with national reach. Netværk for AI i Sundhed is independent; RAIN is hosted by a regional public body. Each convenes clinicians, researchers, developers and decision-makers, and they could come to share events, members or working themes.
  • Connections across the levels. The national network, the faculty bodies and the hospital bodies have largely separate origins. The think tank's link to national policy fora, and RAIT's origin in a regional network, show two ways such connections have formed.
  • Governance for the national network. Once statutes and a board are published on aisundhed.dk, the map can show them as a governance body, as it does for the Aalborg centre's steering committee.
  • Education. The national network is exploring a master's programme in health for data scientists.
  • The 2027 setting. Region Østdanmark, the health councils and Digital Sundhed Danmark are new places where AI-in-health activity could be organised.

In this map

  • Regions. Region Midtjylland's and Region Sjælland's own AI programmes, and Syddansk Sundhedsinnovation's Partnership on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare.
  • Hospitals. The university hospitals as bodies in their own right, and leads at Rigshospitalet, the Technical University Hospital of Greater Copenhagen and Steno Diabetes Center Copenhagen.
  • National bodies. Sundhedsstyrelsen, Dataetisk Råd and Lægemiddelstyrelsen, and the professional and specialist societies.
  • Corrections and additions are welcome. The vault is open on GitHub; every named person's entry is marked distilled from public sources, to be reviewed.
