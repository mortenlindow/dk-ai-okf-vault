DK-AI · Rules register · as of 2 November 2026

Rules and guidance on AI in Denmark

Statute, national guidance, local instructions and institutional input on how AI may be used, in three domains: clinical work in the health service, research, and education. The clinical domain is specific to health. The research and education domains cover all fields; documents written for health research or health training are included alongside them.

About this register

How it was collected

All three domains were collected on 2 November 2026. For clinical use, national authorities, about thirty professional and patient organisations, the five regions, and four of the five regions' public instruction databases were searched for documents that name AI and say what health organisations or staff must, should or may do. Institutional input that binds no one — consultation responses, policy proposals — is included and marked as such. Each document was read at its source; where its text sits behind a staff login, only its existence is recorded. For research and education, national bodies, research funders, the research-ethics committees, the eight universities, and the school-level authorities and organisations were searched the same way; education documents are grouped by the level they apply to. Every card distils at most five provisions in the vault's own words — the source document is always the authority.

Bindinga statute, or an instruction or guideline staff must follow
Guidanceguidance from an authority or body, presented as such
Recommendationfrom an advisory body or professional association
Inputnot authoritative: consultation responses, proposals
Login-walledexistence recorded; text not read

Where to find the data

The register is part of DK-AI, an open knowledge base on GitHub. Each card names its file in the vault's policy folder. The register view also lists documents where AI is a short passage of a wider instruction, documents read but not included, the searches made, and leads still to follow. Corrections and additions: open an issue.
