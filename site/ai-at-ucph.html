AI at UCPH

The University of Copenhagen's AI activity is organised in three layers: coordination at university level, a set of research and education centres, and a European network whose Danish hub sits at the Department of Computer Science. People join the layers. The leaders of six of the centres on this page sit on the UCPH AI steering committee. Since September 2026 a new AI Office, headed by UCPH's first Deputy Prorector for AI, runs a cross-university initiative to 2029.

Taking shape

Units still being clarified

On 27 August 2026 UCPH announced a cross-university AI initiative of DKK 110 million, organised under two missions, AI at UCPH and UCPH with AI, and designed to run for three years before being embedded in the organisation. Its parts are at different stages. This is what UCPH's own pages describe so far.

Education

AI in UCPH education

  • Machine learning og datavidenskab, a bachelor's degree since 2019, belongs to the Faculty of Science and its study board for mathematics and computer science.
  • Kognitions- og datavidenskab, a bachelor's degree in the Faculty of Social Sciences, offers an AI and human-centred computing specialisation.
  • CPDSE, a joint UCPH–SDU centre, works across bachelor, master, PhD and life-long learning in pharmaceutical data science, with a PhD programme and an industry course in AI- and data-driven drug design.
  • The Pioneer Centre recruits PhD students as a cohort across its five partner universities.
  • The ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program supervises across two European countries, with Danish participation through ELLIS Unit Denmark.
