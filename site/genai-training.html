DK-AI · Training register · as of 2 November 2026

Generative AI training for clinicians

Courses, e-learning, webinars, workshops and guides on generative AI — language models, ChatGPT, Copilot and the like — offered by Danish suppliers to clinicians and other health professionals.

See also: AI in Danish Health, a register of the networks, centres and hospital bodies organising AI in health.

About this register

How it was collected

Each offering was read on a public page or document on 2 November 2026. An offering is included when a Danish supplier — public, non-profit or commercial — aims it at clinicians or health professionals and names generative AI in its stated scope. Material behind a staff login is recorded from its public description. Dates are the supplier's own. The register describes what is offered; it does not rate it.

Where to find the data

The register is part of DK-AI, an open knowledge base on GitHub. Each card names its file in the vault's training folder, and the register view lists the offerings read but not included. Suggestions: open an issue.
