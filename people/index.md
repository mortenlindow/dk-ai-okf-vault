# People

Named individuals holding **publicly published professional roles** in the bodies mapped
by this vault.

## What is recorded

Name, published professional title, institutional affiliation, and links to the concepts
where they hold a role. Nothing else.

## What is not recorded

No personal contact details — a functional mailbox belongs on the body, not on a person.
No biographical, personal, political or health information. No inferred seniority,
influence rankings or network-centrality claims. No aggregation across sources into a
profile that is not published anywhere.

A person appears here only if they are named on an **official published page in a named
role** relevant to an in-scope concept, and that page is the entry's `resource:`. Event
speakers without such a role stay as plain text in the event's programme.

Seats and appointment terms live on the **body**, not here. A person entry carries an
undated link.

## Removal

To have an entry removed or corrected, open an issue on this repository. Entries record
only already-public role information, but removal requests are honoured without
requiring a reason.
