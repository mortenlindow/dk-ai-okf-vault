# Scope

A *Digital kaffepause* email course: over five weeks participants receive one email a week with a video in which colleagues around Region Midtjylland show how they use generative AI in their everyday work. It asks how ChatGPT can be used *"til mere end festivaler og opskrifter"* and how to use the tools safely [1]. The course is listed in the region's catalogue of leadership-development programmes and is open to staff as well as leaders; details and registration are on Plan2Learn [1].
