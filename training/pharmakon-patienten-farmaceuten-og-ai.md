# Scope

An article in Pharmakon's series *Evidens for apotekspraksis*, summarising the AI presentations at the International Pharmaceutical Federation's 2024 congress. It covers how AI is used with patients and what the pharmacist's role becomes, evidence-based medicine in a world of generative AI and language models, bias in training data, transparency, data security, and the need for training of the professionals who use the tools [1].

# Notes

Pharmakon describes itself as owned by Danmarks Apotekerforening and organised as a limited company [2]. It is a for-profit form owned by a trade association, so the entry carries the `commercial` tag.
