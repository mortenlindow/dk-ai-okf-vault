# Scope

A session at the RAIN network's AI theme day on 3 December 2025, asking when generative AI is the right answer to a problem. The slides cover how language models work, hallucination and automation bias, the region's SydChat and its guidelines, a checklist for deciding when to use a chatbot, and prompting; the summary describes hands-on prompting exercises without patient data [1][2]. The summary gives 117 participants from clinical specialties, administration, project management, IT and data, and leadership [1].
