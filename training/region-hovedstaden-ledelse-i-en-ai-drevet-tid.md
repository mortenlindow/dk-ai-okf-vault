# Scope

A two-day course for leaders who want to *"arbejde effektivt med generativ AI i din lederrolle"*. Day one introduces generative AI with hands-on exercises on cases from participants' own practice and reflection on its potential and limits. Day two turns to leadership dilemmas, value creation and implementation, ending in a roadmap for the participant's own unit. Patient safety, ethics and professional complacency (*faglig dovenskab*) are named as themes. Participants bring their own computer and need access to an AI chatbot such as ChatGPT or Copilot [1][2].
