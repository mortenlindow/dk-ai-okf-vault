# Scope

How AI can help a general practitioner with instructions, phrases, staff teaching, clinical puzzles and certificates, and how prompts can be improved for these tasks, with practical exercises and exchange of experience. Prior use of AI is an advantage but not a requirement; participants need *"basal forståelse for, hvordan man bruger sprogmodeller (LLM)"*, as this is not covered in detail. The catalogue recommends bringing a computer and having a subscription to a language model, and notes GDPR as a limit on use [1].

# Notes
