# Scope

An introduction to AI in general practice, *"herunder store sprogmodeller (LLM)"*, covering where the technology can be used to advantage, where its limits and pitfalls lie, how it can support both clinic and administration, and the ethical, legal and professional considerations involved. Participants try AI on concrete clinical examples during the course and are asked to bring a phone or computer [1].

An earlier run, course 107 at Lægedage 2025 (10 November 2025), carried the same title [2].

# Notes

Lægedage 2026 runs 9–13 November 2026 at Bella Center, Copenhagen, with 139 courses over five days. The catalogue gives one price per day of attendance, DKK 3,700, with reduced rates for some groups, and lists each course by day, time, audience and course leader.
