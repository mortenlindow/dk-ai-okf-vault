# Scope

A start-up webinar for general practices that have not yet begun using AI. It begins with administrative tasks, gives practical examples from a practice, a short update from PLO on its AI work, and an account of the legal framework and requirements; the secretariat's data consultants offer follow-up sparring [1][2]. The programme notes that practices already using tools such as *"ChatGPT, Claude eller Noteless"* are not its audience [2].

The page marks the webinar as held. No recording or later run is listed on the secretariat's course pages as of 2026-11-02 [1].

# Notes

KAP-S describes itself as *"et sekretariat nedsat af Kvalitets- og Efteruddannelsesudvalget"*, supporting quality development, continuing education, IT and data, and practice development under the general-practice agreement [3].
