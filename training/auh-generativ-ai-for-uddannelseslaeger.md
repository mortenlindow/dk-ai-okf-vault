# Scope

A 57-slide talk given at the hospital's annual meeting for postgraduate medical training, filed under the theme *Kunstig intelligens i et uddannelsesperspektiv*. It explains how generative AI and large language models work, introduces prompting and more advanced prompt techniques, discusses problems in their use, and gives examples from university teaching [1].
