# Scope

A discussion sheet for groups in decentral group-based continuing education (DGE) for general practitioners, to use after listening to a podcast episode on AI in general practice, titled *Fremtiden er ikke på vej, den er her!*. Its questions cover speech-to-text, chatbots, replying to patients' mail, legal and ethical aspects, and patients who consult a chatbot or wearables before a consultation; it names tools such as *"ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude"* [1].

The podcast episode itself is not yet recorded in this vault.
