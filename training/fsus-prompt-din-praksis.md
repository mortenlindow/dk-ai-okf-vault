# Scope

A two-day conference on generative AI in the teaching and clinical supervision of nursing students. A workshop shows how generative AI works, how it differs from other AI, and how it enters the work of educators and health professionals, with chances to try the technology. Further talks cover teaching and supervision *"i en tid med generativ kunstig intelligens"*, including ChatGPT, and concrete classroom uses [1].
