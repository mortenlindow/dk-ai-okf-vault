# Scope

The guide gives staff an overview of what generative AI is, when and how they may use it in their work, which services are approved, and how data security and ethics are handled. A companion page offers prompting advice, from basic questions to more systematic prompt frameworks [1].

The same page describes SydChat, the region's own GPT platform, as available for writing, summarising and planning support, but *"ikke til kliniske beslutninger eller patientvurderinger"*; CPR numbers and health information may not be entered [1]. The guide itself is on the region's intranet and was not opened; this entry is drawn from its public description on [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md)'s page.
