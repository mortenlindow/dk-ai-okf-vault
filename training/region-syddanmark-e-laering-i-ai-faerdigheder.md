# Scope

The course gives *"en grundlæggende forståelse af, hvad generativ AI er"* and of how to use the technology safely and responsibly at work, in line with the EU's requirements for AI competence. The page states that the course is to be completed by anyone who uses generative AI in their work [1].

It is listed on *AI på OUH*, a page maintained by [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md) for staff of Odense University Hospital, Region Syddanmark and SDU, alongside the region's guide to generative AI and its own chat platform, SydChat [1]. The course itself sits on the region's Plan2Learn portal behind a staff login and was not opened.
