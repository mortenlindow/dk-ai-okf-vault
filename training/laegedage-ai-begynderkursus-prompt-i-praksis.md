# Scope

For staff who have never opened ChatGPT or other AI tools, or use them now and then. The course builds a good prompt from the ground up — what works, what does not, and how to refine a question until the answer is usable — starting from familiar tasks: a letter to a patient, an instruction for staff, a reply to an enquiry, a look-up in a guideline [1].
