# Scope

A page for the region's leaders on AI. It notes that AI projects in a regional setting are often high-risk under the EU AI Act and that leaders carry particular responsibility for transparency, risk and ethics [1]. It collects episodes of the region's leadership podcast on AI; recordings from a leadership conference, including a talk titled *"Generativ AI – hvad betyder det for vores arbejdsliv lige nu og i fremtiden?"*; recordings of theme days on AI and leadership; and a link to a webinar series from Væksthus for Ledelse on leading the implementation of generative AI, including sessions on professional leadership and generative AI [1].
