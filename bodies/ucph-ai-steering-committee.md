# Summary

The steering committee of [UCPH AI](/initiatives/ucph-ai.md), described by the initiative
as *"12 professors from the five faculties and two administrative leaders, who have been
appointed for a two-year period based on professional expertise and personal commitment."*
A Chair and a Deputy Chair, appointed from within the committee, hold *"the overarching
responsibility for the initiative."*

This is the **canonical home for who holds which seat and for how long**; the initiative's
own concept carries only the chair and deputy.

Its composition brings together the directors of all four UCPH-anchored AI centres,
academics from each of the five faculties, and the head of UCPH's innovation office as an
observer. It is the setting in which the university's AI leadership meets.
