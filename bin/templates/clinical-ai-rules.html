DK-AI · Rules register · as of 2 November 2026

Rules and guidance on AI in Danish health

Statute, national guidance, regional guidelines, hospital and department instructions, and the input of professional and patient organisations on how AI may be used — in clinical work, in research and in education.

See also: Networks about AI in Danish Health · Generative AI training for clinicians

About this register

How it was collected

For clinical use: on 2 November 2026 national authorities, about thirty professional and patient organisations, the five regions, and four of the five regions' public instruction databases were searched for documents that name AI and say what health organisations or staff must, should or may do. Institutional input that binds no one — consultation responses, policy proposals — is included and marked as such. Each document was read at its source; where its text sits behind a staff login, only its existence is recorded. Every card distils at most five provisions in the vault's own words — the source document is always the authority.

Bindinga statute, or an instruction or guideline staff must follow
Guidanceguidance from an authority or body, presented as such
Recommendationfrom an advisory body or professional association
Inputnot authoritative: consultation responses, proposals
Login-walledexistence recorded; text not read

Where to find the data

The register is part of DK-AI, an open knowledge base on GitHub. Each card names its file in the vault's policy folder. The register view also lists documents where AI is a short passage of a wider instruction, documents read but not included, the searches made, and leads still to follow. Corrections and additions: open an issue.
