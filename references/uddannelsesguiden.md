# Summary

The national guidance portal for Danish education, covering youth education, higher
education, and adult and continuing education, under the tagline *"Viden om og
inspiration til uddannelse og job i Danmark."* (*"Knowledge about and inspiration for education and jobs in Denmark"*)

**This is the designated registry for Danish AI degree programmes.** Under the education
carve-out in `CLAUDE.md` §1, DK-AI records a programme as its own concept only when it
connects two or more concepts. Every other AI degree in Denmark belongs here, where the
listing is complete and kept current, rather than in a vault snapshot that would go stale
within a year.

This makes it the third registry the vault points at, beside the
[Nordic AI Health Map](/references/nordic-ai-health-map.md) for deployed solutions and the
Netværk for AI i Sundhed member list for its members.

# Notes

At capture, the guide lists AI-named programmes at several levels and institutions,
including bachelor programmes titled *Kunstig intelligens*, *Kunstig intelligens og data*,
*Machine learning og datavidenskab* and *Datavidenskab og machine learning*; a master's
titled *Datavidenskab og machine learning*; and a professional bachelor, *Diplomingeniør —
design og anvendelse af kunstig intelligens*. **These are pointed at, not recorded**; the
list is illustrative, not complete.

The professional bachelor is worth noting for the frame. It comes from the
professionshøjskole and engineering-college sector, **which this vault has not
enumerated**. The narrow programme rule keeps that sector out: its single-provider degrees
would only ever be rows.
