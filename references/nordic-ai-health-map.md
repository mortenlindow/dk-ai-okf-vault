# Summary

An external, searchable map of **AI use cases in Nordic publicly financed healthcare**,
published by New Nordics AI. Its stated purpose is discovery and networking: *"Find the
Nordic teams working on similar AI use cases."*

**This is the designated elsewhere for everything DK-AI excludes.** Deployed solutions,
apps and models are deliberately out of scope here — they are mapped there. When a
source page pushes a concrete AI tool at you, link here rather than writing it up.

# Use-case categories

The map classifies initiatives into these categories:

- Admin support – healthcare staff
- Admin support – patient treatment
- Clinical care & diagnostics
- Health system management & quality
- Patient self-service & self-care
- Prevention
- Social welfare
- Workforce & capacity planning

# Notes

**Summary and pointer only.** The canonical content is external and must not be
replicated here — not the initiative rows, not the counts beyond the headline figures
above.

It is nonetheless a **discovery surface** for this vault: read its *organisation* column
for leads on the bodies behind the initiatives, while deliberately ignoring the solution
rows themselves.

*The map's funder and its full set of participating countries are not stated on its front
page. Reporting elsewhere attributes it to a Nordic Council of Ministers eHealth initiative
covering Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden; that is noted here as a lead to
confirm from a primary source.*
