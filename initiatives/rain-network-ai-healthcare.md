# Summary

An **open network for AI in healthcare** in which members *"exchange ideas and knowledge and
inspire solutions using artificial intelligence at hospitals and across the region in
general."* It brings together about 500 professionals from across Denmark — clinicians,
researchers, consultants, technology providers and policymakers — from the public sector,
private companies and civil society, and takes part in national and international AI agendas
for healthcare.

It is anchored in the **Region of Southern Denmark** and run by a partnership of five bodies
from the region and its university, among them [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md). Alongside
[Netværk for AI i Sundhed](/initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md), it is one of two open,
national-reach networks for AI in Danish healthcare recorded in this vault — one regionally
anchored, one independent.

# Activities

Network meetings, announced through a Danish-language newsletter, which is also how members
join. Participation is open to anyone interested in and working with AI in Danish healthcare.

# Notes

The network's page sits among its host's 2022 projects; its founding date is not yet
recorded in this vault.

The named contact is host staff acting as secretariat, so no person concept is kept and her
published direct contact details are not recorded — see the person policy in `CLAUDE.md` §6.
Technology providers take part as members; the network is hosted by a regional public body.

RAIN and **TRAIN Europe** (the Trustworthy and Responsible AI Network) are separate networks.
RAIN's page does not refer to TRAIN; see [coverage](/views/coverage/index.md) for how TRAIN
Europe was read.
