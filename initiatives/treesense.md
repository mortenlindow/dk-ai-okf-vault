# Summary

A **Danish National Research Foundation Centre of Excellence** at UCPH's Department of
Geosciences and Natural Resource Management. It aims to *"revolutionize remote sensing of
woody vegetation by developing new means to characterize and quantify global woody
vegetation dynamics, both inside as well as outside of forests"*, and will do so by
*"devising new artificial intelligence (AI) based algorithms for the next generation of
remotely sensed data"* — characterising species, crown size and height, carbon stocks,
sequestration rates and local usage.

It carries **AI in its own name**, and it sits in geosciences — outside computer science,
health and the humanities — a reminder that Danish public AI capacity extends well beyond
the bodies that describe themselves as AI bodies.
