# Summary

A **politically independent, cross-disciplinary Danish network**, founded in 2025, working
*"to ensure that artificial intelligence in Danish healthcare is developed and implemented
on the basis of clinical needs and with the patient's best interests in view."*

It is the **cross-institutional** public health-AI structure in this vault:
where the bodies at [Aarhus](/initiatives/health-ai-network-au.md) and
[Aalborg](/initiatives/centre-for-ai-in-health-aau.md) sit inside a single faculty, this
one convenes clinicians, researchers, technology developers and decision-makers across
hospitals, universities, companies and public authorities.

The network's own diagnosis is a **mismatch**: many AI solutions are built from what is technologically
possible rather than what is clinically necessary; much of the health service lacks the
competence to assess, procure and implement AI; and technology developers often lack
insight into the clinical, regulatory and organisational conditions their solutions must
work under. It addresses this from two sides — **systematic needs assessment**, with an
explicit focus on health equity *"so that AI solutions do not reinforce existing
inequalities"*, and **bilateral competence building**, in the health service and among
developers alike.

# Structure — working groups

Five working groups, referred to internally as *"organellerne"*. Membership is open: a
network member may join one or more.

* [Behovsafdækning](/initiatives/aisundhed-behovsafdaekning.md) - systematic needs assessment.
* [Læring på tværs](/initiatives/aisundhed-laering-paa-tvaers.md) - cross-disciplinary competence building.
* [Tænketank](/initiatives/aisundhed-taenketank.md) - the clinical voice in AI policy debate.
* [Implementering](/initiatives/aisundhed-implementering.md) - pilot to sustained clinical operation.
* [Sustainability](/initiatives/aisundhed-sustainability.md) - the network's own daily operation.

# Activities

Network meetings, workshops and conferences; building connections between clinical
environments, technical research environments and authorities; contributing evidence-based
perspectives to public debate. It is also *"investigating the possibility of a master's
programme in health aimed at data scientists."*

# Notes

**Governance.** The network describes itself as *"run in the startup phase by a coordination
group with representatives from hospitals, universities, private companies and public
authorities."* The composition of that group, and the network's host and funding
arrangements, are not yet recorded in this vault; the site's pages, re-read on 2026-10-20,
do not list them.

**A network in a period of growth.** Membership rose from 173 to 270 in the week of the
[general assembly of 2026-09-23](/events/2026-09-23-generalforsamling-og-netvaerksmoede.md),
and the public member list grew from 108 to 195 entries over the same days. Membership
stood at 335 on 2026-10-20. A
*generalforsamling* is, in Danish practice, the forum of a membership association. Possible
further evolution: an elected board, published statutes, or a named chair — any of which
would give this vault a governance body to record for the network. The next verification
pass will look for them.

**Members.** The network publishes a searchable member list with names, titles and
organisations. In keeping with the person policy in `CLAUDE.md` §6, members are not given
concepts and the list is not copied here; it is used only as a guide to the organisations
taking part. Participants include commercial organisations; the network itself is
non-profit and politically independent.
