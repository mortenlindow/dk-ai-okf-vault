# Summary

A collaboration between the **radiology departments at Herlev, Gentofte, Frederiksberg and
Bispebjerg hospitals** in the Capital Region of Denmark, formed to establish a common
foundation for developing, clinically validating and implementing AI tools in radiology —
specifically, testing whether new or existing algorithms *perform as expected on Danish
patient data*.

It aspires to become a collective knowledge and competence centre for AI in medical
radiological imaging in the Capital Region, and states an ambition to extend the approach
to radiology departments across Denmark.

It is organised around **one clinical speciality across several hospitals**, with a stated
purpose of *validation on Danish patient data* — a distinct role alongside the research and
convening bodies in this vault.

# Notes

RAIT sits close to this vault's scope boundary and stays **inside** it: the *centre*, its
project office and its cross-hospital collaboration are structure, and are recorded. The
**algorithms it tests are not**, and none is described here. Per the Structure Test, a test
centre is a body that can hold a meeting; the tools passing through it are not.

**Origin.** RAIT was established in **August 2020** across the radiology departments at
Bispebjerg and Frederiksberg and at Herlev and Gentofte, *"som konsekvens af den læring … gennem
samarbejdet i AI-Innovationsnetværket"* (*"as a consequence of the learning … through the collaboration in the AI-Innovationsnetværket"*). It grew out of the
[AI-Innovationsnetværket](/initiatives/ai-innovationsnetvaerket-region-hovedstaden.md). This is
the vault's first recorded case of one AI body giving rise to another. The founding date was
recovered on 2026-09-26 from the network's own November 2020 paper.

Its leadership and funding are not yet recorded in this vault. The centre is reported to
have informed a later national deployment project; that is noted here as a lead to follow
up from a primary source.

Like [CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md), RAIT is anchored in hospitals rather than a
university, and came into this vault through a sweep of hospital-anchored bodies.
