# Summary

The Danish member unit of the [ELLIS](/initiatives/ellis.md) network from 2019, established
by researchers from the University of Copenhagen, the Technical University of Denmark and
the IT University of Copenhagen.

**It no longer exists under this name.** On 2026-03-01 it expanded into
[ELLIS Unit Denmark](/initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md), taking in three further
universities. The file is kept because path is identity and the 2019–2026 unit is a real
part of the landscape's history — see `CLAUDE.md` §5.
