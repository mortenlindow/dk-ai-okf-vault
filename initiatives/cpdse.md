# Summary

*"A joint initiative between UCPH and SDU that integrates data science into pharmaceutical
education."* It trains future drug experts across bachelor, master, PhD and life-long
learning levels, so that pharmaceutical scientists become *"bilinguals speaking both 'data
and drugs'"*. Its vision: *"Data science is an integral core capability in life science
workers and organisations."*

Its work runs along five lines — community, infrastructure, curriculum reform, direct
teaching, and national and international networks — and it works with students, university
teachers, researchers and professionals from the pharmaceutical industry.

AI appears by name in its offering. Its course *AI- & Data-Driven Drug Design – An
Introduction* (University of Copenhagen, 9–13 November 2026) covers AI/ML tools for peptides
and proteins and data analysis using AI/ML, and is open to professionals from the
pharmaceutical and biotech industry.

# Activities

- The *AI- & Data-Driven Drug Design* course above, directed by Professor David Gloriam.
- A PhD programme: five projects selected for funding, announced February 2026.
- Curriculum development, a Student Code Club, a fellow programme, and open teaching and
  research materials on GitHub.

Its own timeline: the vision formed in 2023, the grant was awarded in 2024, and it has worked
in half-year *seasons* since 2025; Season 3 (2026) is titled *"Systematize, Service, Expose"*.
