# Summary

The Danish member unit of the [ELLIS](/initiatives/ellis.md) network, spanning **all six
Danish universities that do AI research**, with 30+ named members. Its focus is
*"fundamental research"* within an interdisciplinary framework.

It spans **six universities** —
alongside [Pioneer](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) and
[CAISA](/initiatives/caisa.md) at five each — and its primary purpose is to connect Danish AI
research to a European structure.

# Succession

**The single home of this succession.** [ELLIS Unit Copenhagen](/initiatives/ellis-unit-copenhagen.md)
was established in **2019** by researchers from UCPH, DTU and ITU. On **2026-03-01** it
expanded into ELLIS Unit Denmark, adding Aalborg University, Aarhus University and the
University of Southern Denmark.

This was an **expansion, not a dissolution**: the same unit widened its membership and took
a national name. The predecessor's file is kept rather than renamed, because path is
identity. The European network has followed suit — `ellis.eu/units/copenhagen` now redirects
through `sites/unit-copenhagen` to `sites/unit-denmark`.

# Activities

Workshops, research programmes, innovation activities, and the
[ELLIS PhD & Postdoc Program](/programmes/ellis-phd-postdoc-program.md), with international
exchanges and cross-sector collaboration.

# Notes

The [Pioneer Centre for AI](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) *"serves as a hub for
ELLIS activities in Denmark"*, which puts the unit's practical centre of gravity at UCPH's
Department of Computer Science even though the unit itself is national. It is tagged
`national` rather than `ucph` for that reason.

Named fellows include [Christian Igel](/people/christian-igel.md) and
[Serge Belongie](/people/serge-belongie.md), both already mapped here, plus Anders Krogh
(UCPH), Lars Kai Hansen (DTU), Sebastian Risi (ITU) and Akhil Arora (AU/UCPH). *The full
membership was not enumerated in this pass, and members are not given concepts — ELLIS
fellowship is a membership grade, not a role.*
