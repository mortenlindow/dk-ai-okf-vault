# Summary

A **cross-public collaboration** *"between the government, KL and Danske Regioner"* whose
mandate is to *"support that the Danish public sector becomes world-leading in the
application of artificial intelligence."*

It coordinates implementation of the
[tripartite large-scale AI agreement](/funding/offentlig-ai-aftale-2026.md), and it is the
first of the fora named by the [Tænketank](/initiatives/aisundhed-taenketank.md) of the
national health-AI network as a setting for its policy input. It therefore connects the
government's delivery programme with the health-AI community's policy work.

# Targets

Five goals are published, with these the most concrete:

- AI deployment to free **at least 50 million hours — around 30,000 full-time equivalents —
  across the public sector by 2035**.
- **At least 80% of public authorities using AI by 2030** where it creates value.
- No more than 20% of authorities citing unclear legal frameworks as a barrier.
- Simpler interactions for citizens and businesses; public employees who feel supported.

# Notes

An **expert group** has been appointed by the government, KL and Danske Regioner to give
strategic and professional advice to the taskforce, with representatives from the public
sector, business and research. Its composition is not yet recorded in this vault.

The taskforce's site describes its mandate and targets; its chair and members are not yet
recorded in this vault. The ministry publishes a commissorium for the taskforce, which is a
natural next source to read.
