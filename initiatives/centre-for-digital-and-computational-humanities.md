# Summary

A *"research hub dedicated to supporting and advancing work at the intersection of the
humanities and digital technologies"* at UCPH's Faculty of Humanities. Its mission is to
integrate digital and computational methods into humanities research, and it explicitly
*"provide[s] access to expertise in artificial intelligence and data science"* — alongside
training and research infrastructure.

It is the **second humanities-anchored AI body** at UCPH, beside the
[Centre for Language Technology](/initiatives/centre-for-language-technology.md), and the
two divide the ground: CST does NLP research for Danish, CDCH provides AI and data-science
capacity to humanities researchers who are not themselves methodologists.
