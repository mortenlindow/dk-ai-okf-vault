# Initiatives

**The collection target.** Named undertakings whose reason for existing is AI: centres,
strategic initiatives, national consortia, voluntary networks, clubs, working groups.

All one `type: Initiative`, distinguished by a `| Form |` row — because "centre",
"network", "consortium" and "initiative" are self-applied labels that are often plural
and change with a rebrand, and path is identity.

Flat by design: any sub-foldering would use a contested axis (by form) or an unstable one
(by host — CAISA has five). Grouping is free in this index and in tags.

## University of Copenhagen

* [AI Office (University of Copenhagen)](ucph-ai-office.md) - group unit headed by the Deputy Prorector for AI, running a cross-university AI initiative, 2026–2029, with an AI task force announced.
* [UCPH AI](ucph-ai.md) - the university's cross-faculty strategic initiative; coordinates rather than researches, and its steering committee is where the four centre directors below sit together.
* [Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence](pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) - national five-university research centre hosted at DIKU; seven collaboratories.
* [CAISA](caisa.md) - National Centre for AI in Society; government-financed five-university consortium, directed from UCPH.
* [SCIENCE AI Centre](science-ai-centre.md) - faculty-level centre at SCIENCE, weighting education alongside research.
* [Centre for Language Technology](centre-for-language-technology.md) - NLP for Danish, anchored in the humanities.
* [Centre for Digital and Computational Humanities](centre-for-digital-and-computational-humanities.md) - AI and data-science expertise for humanities researchers.
* [CORS² — Copenhagen Centre for Regulatory Science](cors.md) - pharmacy; AI in drug-safety research.
* [TreeSense](treesense.md) - Centre of Excellence in geosciences using AI-based algorithms for global remote sensing.
* [CPDSE](cpdse.md) - joint UCPH–SDU centre integrating data science, including AI, into pharmaceutical education.
* [ELLIS Unit Copenhagen](ellis-unit-copenhagen.md) - 2019–2026; superseded by ELLIS Unit Denmark.

## AI in health

### National

* [Netværk for AI i Sundhed](netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md) - politically independent cross-institutional network, founded 2025. Its five working groups:
  * [Behovsafdækning](aisundhed-behovsafdaekning.md) - where AI creates the greatest clinical value.
  * [Læring på tværs](aisundhed-laering-paa-tvaers.md) - competence building across clinicians, developers, lawyers and patients.
  * [Tænketank](aisundhed-taenketank.md) - the clinical voice in AI policy debate.
  * [Implementering](aisundhed-implementering.md) - pilot to sustained clinical operation.
  * [Sustainability](aisundhed-sustainability.md) - the network's own operation and fundraising.

### University-anchored

* [Health-AI Network (Aarhus University)](health-ai-network-au.md) - faculty research network, founded 2026; co-chaired, with open membership.
* [Centre for AI in Health (Aalborg University)](centre-for-ai-in-health-aau.md) - interdisciplinary research centre with a head, deputy, steering committee and published rules of procedure; partnered with Region Nordjylland.

### Regional

* [RAIN – Network for AI in Healthcare](rain-network-ai-healthcare.md) - open network of about 500 professionals, anchored in the Region of Southern Denmark.

### Hospital-anchored

* [CAI-X — Centre for Clinical Artificial Intelligence](cai-x.md) - joint Odense University Hospital / SDU centre, spanning a hospital and two faculties.
* [AI-Innovationsnetværket (Region Hovedstaden)](ai-innovationsnetvaerket-region-hovedstaden.md) - informal cross-hospital AI network, 2019. Status unknown since 2020.
* [RAIT — Radiological Artificial Intelligence Test Centre](rait.md) - Capital Region radiology departments validating AI on Danish patient data. Founded 2020 out of the network above.

### Cluster

* [Netværk om AI (Danish Life Science Cluster)](dlsc-netvaerk-om-ai.md) - public–private development network across the cluster's regional hubs.
