# Summary

Works on what often decides whether an AI solution creates value: **the transition from
trial to sustained clinical operation**. Its premise is that even well-documented tools
*"run aground if they are not anchored organisationally, fitted into workflows, and
followed by clear responsibility and governance structures, training, and ongoing
evaluation of effect and safety after adoption."*

The group collects and shares concrete implementation experience across departments and
regions, so that the concern for health equity *"follows the solution all the way from
pilot to routine"*.
