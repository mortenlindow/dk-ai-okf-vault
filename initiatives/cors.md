# Summary

A regulatory-science centre at UCPH's Department of Pharmacy, working across
pharmacoepidemiology, drug safety and regulatory affairs. Its **Drug Safety Group** is the
part that brings it into this vault: it *"harnesses large and heterogeneous biomedical
real-world data, integrating cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence and intelligent
automation"*, and the centre advertises supervision in *"AI driven Pharmacovigilance and
Pharmacoepidemiology"*.

Among the 34 research centres at UCPH's Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences, it is the
one whose published research names AI — see the note below on how AI-in-health activity is
organised at UCPH.

# Notes

**Where AI sits in the centre.** CORS² is a regulatory-science centre, and AI appears in the
stated method of one of its three groups. It is included because its own pages name AI as
part of its research.

**Context.** This centre was identified by reading all 34 research centres at the Faculty
of Health and Medical Sciences: 4 with direct reference to the Dean and 30 embedded in
departments. At UCPH, AI-in-health activity is currently organised mainly through
individual researchers, [DIKU](/organisations/academic/diku.md), the
[Pioneer Centre](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) and the Capital Region hospitals,
with CORS² as the faculty centre whose research names AI. Possible further evolution: a
faculty-level structure with AI in health as its stated subject. See
[views/coverage/index.md](/views/coverage/index.md) for how the centres were read.
