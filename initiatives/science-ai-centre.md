# Summary

A **faculty-level AI centre** at UCPH's Faculty of Science, dedicated to *"foster the
excellence in education and research in artificial intelligence (AI) at the Faculty of
Science and at the rest of the University of Copenhagen."*

It works by *"facilitating cross-disciplinary collaborations across faculties,
departments, and industrial and governmental partners"* — pushing AI research forward,
educating the next generation of AI experts, and applying AI to societal challenges from
healthcare to city planning.

It presents itself as an **umbrella** rather than a research group: a *"single point of
entry"* coordinating existing and future AI activity across the faculty, on three pillars —
**education** (new specialisations and programmes), **innovation** (supporting Danish
industry), and **research** (foundational and interdisciplinary, spanning natural sciences,
humanities and social sciences).

Unlike [Pioneer Centre for AI](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) and
[CAISA](/initiatives/caisa.md), which are national consortia hosted at UCPH, this centre's
remit is internal to the university, with education weighted alongside research. Opened in
2018, it predates the Pioneer Centre (2021) and CAISA (2025).

The founding date and Christian Igel's title of CEO come from the faculty's own account of

steering-committee roster lists him as "Director of SCIENCE AI Centre".
