# Summary

A **national consortium** researching artificial intelligence in society, financed by the
Danish state and drawing together five universities in close collaboration with the
[Pioneer Centre for AI](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md).

It states that it *"places citizens at the center of our mission"* and conducts
*"pioneering interdisciplinary research"* to advise decision-makers on developing and
implementing AI responsibly — *"ensuring it drives sustainable growth, strengthens
democracy, and enhances digital self-determination."*

Its interdisciplinary framing is deliberate: understanding AI is presented as requiring
insight into human and societal impact, not only technical expertise.
