# Summary

**UCPH AI** is the University of Copenhagen's **strategic initiative** — its own framing,
and not a research centre — coordinating the university's AI research, education and
innovation activities. Its stated ambition is to strengthen UCPH's position as *"a hub
for foundational AI research and the responsible application of AI within scientific and
societal domains."*

It works in two directions: inward, fostering collaboration across faculties and
administrative units; and outward, serving as *"a gateway for external stakeholders — from
businesses to research communities, public institutions, and policymakers."*

Structurally it sits **above** UCPH's AI centres rather than alongside them. The
directors of all four UCPH-anchored AI centres hold seats on its steering committee, which
makes that committee the point where the university's AI leadership actually convenes.

# Notes

The **initiative / centre distinction is the initiative's own**, stated on its front page,
and it is load-bearing for this vault: UCPH AI coordinates, while
[Pioneer Centre for AI](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md),
[CAISA](/initiatives/caisa.md), [SCIENCE AI Centre](/initiatives/science-ai-centre.md) and
[Centre for Language Technology](/initiatives/centre-for-language-technology.md) do
research.

*The initiative's budget arrangements and the steering committee's formal mandate are not
yet recorded in this vault; the site does not describe them as of this capture.*

**The AI Office.** Since 1 September 2026 UCPH also has a Deputy Prorector for AI, heading a
new [AI Office](/initiatives/ucph-ai-office.md) and a cross-university AI initiative running to
2029. The two are described on separate pages; how they relate is not yet recorded in this
vault.
