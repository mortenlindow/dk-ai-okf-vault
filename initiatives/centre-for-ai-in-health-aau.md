# Summary

An **interdisciplinary research centre** at Aalborg University's health faculty that
*"develops and applies AI solutions in healthcare – from research to clinical practice"*,
bringing AI research and clinical practice together to create real-world impact.

Its stated priorities are decision support, workflow optimisation and effective use of
data, alongside an explicit emphasis on data security, personal-data protection, ethics
and transparency.

It has a named head, a deputy, an administrator, a steering committee and published rules
of procedure. The AI-in-health bodies in this vault take a range of organisational forms,
from this centre's structure to the co-chaired, open-membership networks at Aarhus and
nationally.

# Activities

Interdisciplinary workshops, co-creation of research projects, hackathons and
knowledge-sharing activities. A two-day strategic workshop was held on 29–30 September to
align participants on shared priorities and research directions.

*The year of that workshop, and the centre's founding date, are not stated on the page.*
No event concept was created: the Event gate in `CLAUDE.md` §4 requires a date, and only
a day and month are published.

# Notes

The centre's own framing — *"develops and applies AI solutions"* — points at the boundary
this vault draws. The **centre** is in scope as a structure of people with governance and
partners; the **solutions** it produces are not, and are not described here.

The faculty also designates *AI and Health Data Science* as a **research strength**, naming
Martin Bøgsted and Lasse Riis Østergaard as its contacts. A research strength is a
thematic designation rather than a body with governance, so it is recorded here rather
than given a concept of its own.
