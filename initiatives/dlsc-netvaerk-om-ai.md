# Summary

A **development network** within
[Danish Life Science Cluster](/organisations/funders/danish-life-science-cluster.md),
bringing together *"offentlige og private aktører"* (public and private actors) — municipalities, hospitals, regions,
companies and educational institutions — who work with, or plan to work with, AI in
healthcare. Its stated aim is to *"danner grundlag for innovative og samskabte løsninger
til sundhedssektoren"*: to form the basis for innovative, co-created solutions for the
health sector.

It is the **most explicitly public–private** of the health-AI bodies mapped here, and it
operates through the cluster's regional hubs rather than from a single institution.

# Activities

Four network meetings a year, physical or virtual, run through the cluster's regional hubs.
Past sessions have covered ethics, legal frameworks and operational experience with AI.

# Notes

The host cluster is a **non-profit with a public mandate**, so the network passes the
Public/Non-profit Test despite substantial corporate participation. Its company members and
partners are named in prose where relevant and given no concepts.

The named contact is cluster staff acting as secretariat rather than network leadership, so
no person concept was created, and her published direct contact details are deliberately
not recorded — see the person policy in `CLAUDE.md` §6.
