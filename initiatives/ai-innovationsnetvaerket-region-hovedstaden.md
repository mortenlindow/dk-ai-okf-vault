# Summary

> **Dated source.** Everything below comes from the network's own paper to Region
> Hovedstaden's leadership, dated **November 2020**. Its activity since then is not yet
> recorded in this vault.

An **informal network across Region Hovedstaden** — *"en uformel struktur på tværs af Region
Hovedstaden"* — of clinicians, technicians, managers and researchers. Its stated vision was
to improve the conditions for developing and implementing new technology: solving concrete
problems for AI projects, and improving the general framework for AI, including
infrastructure and access to and sharing of data.

The network is also where another AI body began. [RAIT](/initiatives/rait.md) was
established in August 2020 *"som konsekvens af den læring … gennem samarbejdet i
AI-Innovationsnetværket"* — *"as a consequence of the learning … through the collaboration
in the AI-Innovationsnetværket."* It is the first case in this vault of one AI body giving
rise to another.

# Structure

One of **three thematic innovation networks** facilitated by the region's *Enhed for
Sundhedsforskning og Innovation* in *Center for Regional Udvikling*. The other two covered
rapid diagnostics and digital services. The networks were initiated by a group of regional
leaders who took part in an *"Innovation Leadership Challenge"* in autumn 2018. Each had a
network leader employed by the region.

# Activities

A November 2020 paper to the region's leadership with **seven recommendations** for
strengthening the conditions for AI in the region. These covered a shared development
platform, access to and sharing of data, a proposed AI education programme, career paths
for non-clinical researchers, and cloud hosting of health data.

It also records that network members advised on the region's **DKK 3m AI pool for 2020**,
distributed to small AI projects, and that the regional council approved funding in autumn
2020 for an **AI-wiki** the network initiated.

# Notes

**Source.** The network's public record is its **own paper**, filed in the region's

The paper names several regional bodies that are **not AI bodies** and get no concepts:
Digital Styregruppe, Digital Sundhed Øst (across Region Hovedstaden and Region Sjælland),
CIMT, Center for Regional Udvikling and CAMES. Its flagship-project examples describe
specific AI tools and commercial partners; per the Structure Test those are **out of scope**
and are not recorded.

From 2027-01-01 its region becomes
[Region Østdanmark](/organisations/health/region-oestdanmark.md).
