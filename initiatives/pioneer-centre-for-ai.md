# Summary

A **national AI research centre** hosted by UCPH's Department of Computer Science and
co-led with four partner universities. It *"focuses on fundamental research, and within an
interdisciplinary framework, develops platforms, methods, and practices addressing
society's greatest challenges."*

It is simultaneously a centre and a five-university consortium; per the type rules in
`CLAUDE.md` that is one concept with a partner list, not two.

# Structure — collaboratories

Seven thematic collaboratories, each with named leads. Held as a table rather than as
seven concepts: they are internal research groupings, not bodies with their own governance
or public identity. Promote a row the moment one acquires either.

|---|---|
| Networks and Graphs | Roberta Sinatra, Sune Lehmann Jørgensen |

# Activities

| Programme | Level | Note |
|---|---|---|
| PhD cohort across the five partner universities | PhD | Not a named programme. PhDs are individual positions employed at their host university, forming *"a cohort of students from across the centre's 5 partner-universities."* |
| Summer research internships with CalTech | internship | A *"formal program"* with a US university; not a course of study. |

The centre runs **no named degree or PhD programme**. Its cross-university PhD cohort is
organising, not a programme, and stays a row. For master's study it points students at
external Danish programmes.

# Notes
