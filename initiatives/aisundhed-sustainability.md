# Summary

Ensures the network functions day to day: internal and external communication, planning
and running network meetings, **fundraising** for the network's activities, and
coordination across the other working groups. It is described as *"the connecting link
that makes it possible for the other groups to concentrate on their professional work."*

Its presence is a governance signal in itself: a volunteer network that has staffed its own
operations separately is one that expects to persist.
