# Summary

Contributes **the health-professional voice** to Danish and international debate about AI.
Where many current initiatives treat AI as a general efficiency tool, the think tank holds
that healthcare *"places particular demands on evidence, patient safety, ethics and
equality"*.

It delivers policy briefs, consultation responses, opinion pieces and direct input to
relevant fora.

# Target fora

Named by the group as the fora it feeds into:

- Den Digitale Taskforce for kunstig intelligens
- Dataetisk Råd
- Sundhedsstyrelsen (Danish Health Authority)
- Lægemiddelstyrelsen (Danish Medicines Agency)
- Folketingets sundhedsudvalg (the Danish Parliament's health committee)
- Professional organisations
- European fora on the **AI Act** and the **European Health Data Space**

*None of these bodies has been researched or given a concept yet; they are named here as
published targets, and are leads for a later pass.*
