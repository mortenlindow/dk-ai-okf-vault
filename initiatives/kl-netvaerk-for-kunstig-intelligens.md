# Summary

A network run by [KL](/organisations/public/kl.md) for **municipalities that want to follow
and contribute to** the development and testing of AI solutions in municipal service.

Its framing is distinctly municipal: technology and AI as a response to pressure on
welfare, economy, climate and recruitment, while safeguarding *"retssikkerhed,
datasikkerhed og digital suverænitet"* — legal certainty, data security and **digital
sovereignty**. That last term does not appear in any of the research-side bodies mapped in
this vault, and marks the municipal sector's distinct concern.

# Activities

Four in-person meetings a year with workshops, experience-sharing and keynotes.
Published themes are generative AI in operations, documented use cases, AI regulation
and ethics, digital sovereignty, and international examples.

Membership is **paid**, by annual subscription tiered by headcount (DKK 8,000 for an
individual; DKK 16,000 for up to five employees; DKK 24,000 for up to twelve).

# Notes

A paid subscription does not affect scope: KL is the municipalities' own interest
organisation, the network's participants are public authorities, and the fee funds the
network rather than a vendor. It passes the Public/Non-profit Test.

The two named contacts are **KL staff acting as secretariat**, not network leadership, so
no person concepts were created.

*The network is stated to continue into 2026; its founding date is not yet recorded in this vault.*
