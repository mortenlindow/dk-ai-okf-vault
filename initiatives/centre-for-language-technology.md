# Summary

A UCPH research centre for **language technology and Natural Language Processing,
primarily for Danish**, housed in the Department of Nordic Studies and Linguistics at the
Faculty of Humanities. It describes language technology as *"an interdisciplinary subject
[that] includes the study of language as well as the study of IT."*

It is the humanities-anchored member of UCPH's AI centre cluster, with a specific language
as its subject rather than AI methods in general — a natural home for Danish-language AI
infrastructure questions.
