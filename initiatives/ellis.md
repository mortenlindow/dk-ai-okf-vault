# Summary

> **Foreign-body stub.** Admitted under the Denmark Test in `CLAUDE.md` §1 solely because
> Danish bodies are members. It is **not a research subject for this vault** and is never
> parsed in depth — it exists so that
> [ELLIS Unit Denmark](/initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md) has something to attach to.

A pan-European network of excellence in machine learning, connecting researchers across
**42 units and 2 institutes at 44 sites in 17 countries**, with more than 2,000 members
*(as of 2026-09-22)*.
