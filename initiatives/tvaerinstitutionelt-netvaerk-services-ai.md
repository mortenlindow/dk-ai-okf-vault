# Summary

A **cross-institutional research network** based at Roskilde University, formed to
*"investigate Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its implications for service organisations
in both the private and public sectors"* through interdisciplinary research, curriculum
integration, workshops, seminars and publications.

It ran for three years and **has ended**. It is retained here because it is part of the
landscape's history: a completed network is evidence of where AI organising has already
been tried, and its participants are leads for successor structures.
