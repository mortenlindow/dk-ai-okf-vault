# Summary

The AI partnership of [Syddansk Sundhedsinnovation](/organisations/health/syddansk-sundhedsinnovation.md),
which describes itself as the region's and hospitals' entrance for data and technical integration.
Under this heading the centre helps hospitals identify clinical needs that AI might support, scopes
projects and assembles partners for them, and builds links between clinical operations and the
companies that develop AI for healthcare [1].

Its standing structure is the coordination of [RAIN](/initiatives/rain-network-ai-healthcare.md),
which the centre runs together with Regional IT, the Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute at SDU and
[CAI-X](/initiatives/cai-x.md); through RAIN, regional stakeholders meet three to four times a
year on clinically focused topics [1]. The centre has also hosted national AI-themed conferences,
and offers to act as work-package leader or partner in AI projects, from national coordination and
validation to implementation and pilot test sites [1].
