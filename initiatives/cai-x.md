# Summary

The **Danish Centre for Clinical Artificial Intelligence**, a *"knowledge and competence
centre"* established jointly by the Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute at the University of
Southern Denmark and Odense University Hospital.

Its purpose is to connect the hospital's clinicians with the university's engineers and
researchers, and with private companies, so that AI solutions address real clinical
workflows. It involves **all clinical specialties** at the hospital.

Structurally it is the **hospital–university hybrid** in the Danish landscape: where the
Aarhus and Aalborg bodies sit inside a faculty and the national network sits above
institutions, CAI-X is jointly owned by a hospital and two faculties, with a steering group
drawn from both.

# Activities

Support with scoping AI projects, assistance with funding applications, project development
support, and visibility for research outputs. It cooperates with further SDU faculties,
other hospitals, research centres, councils, and private organisations in Denmark and
internationally.
