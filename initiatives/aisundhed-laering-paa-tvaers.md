# Summary

Builds bridges between professions by supporting competence development among **all** the
actors involved in responsible use of AI in healthcare: data scientists and developers who
need clinical insight; health professionals who must assess and apply AI tools
competently; lawyers working with the regulatory frameworks; and **patients, who should
have real grounds to understand and take part in decisions about AI in their own
treatment**.

The group maps existing educational provision, identifies gaps, and works to ensure
relevant actors have the necessary competences.
