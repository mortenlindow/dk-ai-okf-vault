# Summary

Identifies systematically **where AI can create the greatest clinical value** in Danish
healthcare, starting from concrete clinical needs rather than from available technology,
with a particular focus that AI solutions *"contribute to greater health equality, not
less"*.

Its work is intended to form the basis for prioritising development efforts and for
dialogue with technical development environments about where effort actually makes sense.
