# Summary

A **group unit** at the University of Copenhagen, headed by the university's first Deputy
Prorector for AI, which runs *"a major, cross-university AI initiative running from 2026 to
2029."* The initiative *"aims partly to improve the University's use of AI in research,
education and administration and partly to support University of Copenhagen researchers'
contributions to how society can deal with AI."*

UCPH organises the initiative under **two strategic missions**:

- **AI at UCPH** — when and how AI should, and should not, be part of research and PhD
  programmes, teaching and examination, skills development, administration, and secure and
  lawful use.
- **UCPH with AI** — the university's role in a society and job market changing with AI:
  research on AI's impact, new demands for education, verification and certification, and
  questions of digital sovereignty, climate impact, ethics and regulation.

The structure is designed to be temporary: *"The new structure is intended to run for three
years, after which UCPH expects the initiatives to be implemented in the organisation."*

# Notes

**Relation to UCPH AI.** The university also has [UCPH AI](/initiatives/ucph-ai.md), a
strategic initiative with its own [steering committee](/bodies/ucph-ai-steering-committee.md),
described on `ai.ku.dk`. The AI Office and UCPH AI are described on separate pages, and how
they relate is not yet recorded in this vault.

UCPH's announcement names the [Pioneer Centre for AI](/initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md)
and [CAISA](/initiatives/caisa.md) among the university's existing strengths in AI research.
