# Summary

A combined **general assembly and network meeting** of
[Netværk for AI i Sundhed](/initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md), open to current and
prospective members alike.

The *generalforsamling* is the part with a governance dimension: in Danish practice it is
the forum in which a membership association adopts its statutes and elects its board.

# Outcome

The site marks the event as held (*"Denne begivenhed er allerede afholdt"* (*"This event has already been held"*)). Membership stood
at 270 five days later, up from 173 the week before. The results of the general assembly —
statutes adopted, board elected — are not yet published on the site as of 2026-09-28, and
are the natural next thing to record here.

# Notes

The session list names the needs-assessment group **"Behovsbanken"**; the working-group page
calls it **"Behovsafdækning"**. Both names are recorded; the group may be evolving its name
or its main output.
