# View — UCPH

AI activity at or led by the University of Copenhagen, assembled from every concept tagged
`ucph`. A lens, not a home: every concept below lives in its class folder.

## The shape of it

UCPH's AI activity is organised in three layers.

- **Coordination.** [UCPH AI](../../initiatives/ucph-ai.md) is a strategic initiative — in
  its own words, not a centre — coordinating AI research, education and innovation across
  the faculties. Since 1 September 2026 the university also has a Deputy Prorector for AI,
  heading the [AI Office](../../initiatives/ucph-ai-office.md) and a cross-university AI
  initiative of DKK 110m running to 2029, with an AI task force, three AI labs and a new
  governance structure announced.
- **Research centres.** Two national consortia hosted at UCPH
  ([Pioneer Centre for AI](../../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md), [CAISA](../../initiatives/caisa.md)),
  a faculty centre ([SCIENCE AI Centre](../../initiatives/science-ai-centre.md), opened in
  2018), and centres anchored in the humanities, geosciences and pharmacy.
- **European.** [ELLIS Unit Denmark](../../initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md), whose Danish
  hub is the Pioneer Centre at DIKU.

The layers are joined by people. The directors of the four UCPH-anchored AI centres sit on
the [UCPH AI Steering Committee](../../bodies/ucph-ai-steering-committee.md) alongside
academics from all five faculties, which makes the committee the setting where the
university's AI leadership meets.

## Initiatives

### Coordination and AI centres

* [AI Office (University of Copenhagen)](../../initiatives/ucph-ai-office.md) - headed by the Deputy Prorector for AI; runs the 2026–2029 AI initiative under two missions, *AI at UCPH* and *UCPH with AI*.
* [UCPH AI](../../initiatives/ucph-ai.md) - cross-faculty strategic initiative for AI research, education and innovation.
* [Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence](../../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md) - national five-university centre hosted at DIKU; seven collaboratories.
* [CAISA](../../initiatives/caisa.md) - National Centre for AI in Society; state-financed five-university consortium, directed from UCPH.
* [SCIENCE AI Centre](../../initiatives/science-ai-centre.md) - faculty centre, 2018, on education, innovation and research.
* [Centre for Language Technology](../../initiatives/centre-for-language-technology.md) - NLP for Danish, anchored in the humanities.
* [Centre for Digital and Computational Humanities](../../initiatives/centre-for-digital-and-computational-humanities.md) - AI and data-science expertise for humanities research.
* [ELLIS Unit Copenhagen](../../initiatives/ellis-unit-copenhagen.md) - 2019–2026, UCPH/DTU/ITU; continued as the national [ELLIS Unit Denmark](../../initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md).

### AI across other disciplines

* [TreeSense](../../initiatives/treesense.md) - DNRF Centre of Excellence in geosciences; AI-based algorithms for remote sensing of global tree resources.
* [CORS² — Copenhagen Centre for Regulatory Science](../../initiatives/cors.md) - pharmacy; AI in drug-safety research.
* [CPDSE](../../initiatives/cpdse.md) - joint UCPH–SDU centre for pharmaceutical data science education; offers a course in AI- and data-driven drug design.

## Education

UCPH's AI degrees are each offered by a single faculty, so they are recorded as rows on the
body that offers them:

* **Machine learning og datavidenskab** (bachelor, since 2019) - on the [Faculty of Science](../../organisations/academic/science-ku.md), drawing on courses from both DIKU and Mathematical Sciences.
* **Kognitions- og datavidenskab** (bachelor), with an AI specialisation - on [SAMF](../../organisations/academic/samf-ku.md).
* A PhD cohort across the Pioneer Centre's five universities - on the [Pioneer Centre](../../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md).
* [CPDSE](../../initiatives/cpdse.md), a joint UCPH–SDU centre, works across bachelor, master, PhD and life-long learning in pharmaceutical data science, with a PhD programme and an industry course in AI- and data-driven drug design.

UCPH also offers *Sundhed og informatik* (bachelor and master) in health informatics and
digital health; as its stated subject is digital health rather than AI, it sits outside the
programme rule.

## AI and health at UCPH

AI-in-health activity at UCPH is organised mainly through individual researchers — two of
the steering committee's members come from the Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences — and
through [DIKU](../../organisations/academic/diku.md), the
[Pioneer Centre](../../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md), [CORS²](../../initiatives/cors.md)
and the Capital Region hospitals, where many UCPH clinical academics hold joint appointments.
[Rigshospitalet](../../organisations/health/rigshospitalet.md) hosts surgical AI research.

## Possible further evolution

Patterns in the landscape suggest directions it could take:

- **The AI Office's components taking shape.** The announced AI task force, three AI labs and
  new governance structure may each gain a published mandate and membership — and with them,
  a described relationship to UCPH AI and its steering committee.
- **A health-anchored AI structure.** A centre or network with AI in health as its stated
  subject would sit naturally alongside the four centre directors on the steering committee,
  and between the Pioneer Centre's methods research and the hospitals' clinical work.
- **Links to the national health-AI network.**
  [Netværk for AI i Sundhed](../../initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md) convenes
  hospitals, universities and authorities, and its working groups cover needs assessment,
  cross-disciplinary learning and implementation — areas that meet UCPH AI's own brief.
- **AI-in-health education.** The national network is exploring a master's programme for
  data scientists in health; UCPH's machine-learning and health-informatics degrees are
  adjacent to that space.
- **The 2027 regional reform.** From 2027-01-01 the Capital Region hospitals belong to
  [Region Østdanmark](../../organisations/health/region-oestdanmark.md), which may shape how
  university–hospital AI collaboration is organised.

For how this map itself is expected to grow, see [coverage](../coverage/index.md).

## How centres were read

Every one of UCPH's 118 listed research centres was read against the rule that a centre is
included where **AI or ML is a named focus** on its own pages. Four are included beyond the
dedicated AI centres: CST, CDCH, CORS² and TreeSense. The rest — including SODAS, CeBIL,
iCourts, CIIR, BARC and CTS, several of which work with data and computation — are outside
the current rule rather than outside the landscape. The reading is recorded in
[coverage](../coverage/index.md).
