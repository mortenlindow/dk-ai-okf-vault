# View — Rules and guidance on AI in research and education

A register of **rules, guidance and institutional input on AI in research and in education** in
Denmark — the national research-integrity and ethics framework, research funders, the universities,
and, for education, every level from the folkeskole to specialist medical training. It is the
companion to [Rules and guidance on clinical AI](../clinical-ai-rules/index.md); each entry is a
`Policy` concept in `policy/`, with `Domain` set to `research`, `education` or both, and `Level`
giving the education level. The `Force` of each entry is shown in italics, as in the clinical view.

Read on public pages and documents on 2026-11-02, including the public instruction databases of four
of the five regions for hospital rules on research and education.

## Listed, not filed

* **Worked examples on imaging data** (De Videnskabsetiske Komitéer) — one example concerns a hospital department evaluating a CE-marked AI system against doctors in breast-cancer screening, to help decide whether such a study is health-data-science research.
* ***5 tips til AI-drevet forskning i sundhedsvæsenet*** on the committees' site — written by an individual expert, not issued by the committees.
* **Clinical-placement frameworks in Region Sjælland's psychiatry** (Uddannelse i Psykiatrien) — each has a section barring personal data from translation and AI tools such as Copilot and ChatGPT during placement: for nursing students and for other professional-bachelor students (*higher education*), and for social- and health-care assistant trainees (*vocational*).
* **Strategi for bioanalytikerfaget** (Sjællands Universitetshospital, Klinisk Biokemisk Afdeling) — a sub-goal that future development and research projects should consider AI.

## Read, not filed

* **Exam order for university programmes** (BEK nr. 1121 of 2025) — does not name AI; rules on cheating, and so on generative AI, are left to each university.
* **Amendment to the committee act on risk-based review** (LSF 132, 2025/26) — names research in AI once, as an example of an ethical dilemma.
* **The folkeskole exam order** (BEK nr. 678 of 2025) and the vocational exam and social- and health-care training orders — do not name AI.
* **The ministry's package against AI cheating** (6 August 2026) and the announcement of a national AI strategy for the school and education sector (28 September 2026) — news items; see the [hf agreement](../../policy/aftale-aendring-af-stoerre-skriftlig-opgave-sso-hf.md).
* **Generativ AI: Beslutningsgrundlag til erhvervsskoler og gymnasier** — states that it represents the views of a network of information-security leaders, not the ministry's.
* **EVA's surveys** of pupils' AI use and of assessment practice — research, not normative.
* **KL's guide on generative AI for municipalities** — not addressed to schools; **KL's inspiration catalogue** of eight school cases — mainly examples.
* **Sundhedsstyrelsen's material on the future of specialist medical training** (2025) — AI appears as a stakeholder category only.
* **Danske Universiteter** — an op-ed co-signed by its chair (October 2026); no formal principles found.
* **Aarhus University, Faculty of Health** — news items (2024) on exempting the medicine and dentistry bachelor projects and theses from the university's easing of AI rules for a period.
* **University of Copenhagen, student declaration of generative-AI use** — read only as a copy hosted by a third party, so not filed.
