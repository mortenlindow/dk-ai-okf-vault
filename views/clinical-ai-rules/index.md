# View — Rules and guidance on clinical AI

A register of **rules, guidance and institutional input on the clinical use of AI in Denmark**,
from statute and national authorities down to single hospital departments, professional
associations and patient organisations. Each entry is one document, filed as a `Policy` concept
in `policy/`; the rules for admission are in `CLAUDE.md` §4.

Every entry carries a `Force` row, shown here in italics:

- *binding* — a statute, or an instruction or guideline staff must follow;
- *guidance* — an authority's or body's guidance, presented as such;
- *recommendation* — recommendations from an advisory body or professional association;
- *input* — institutional input that binds no one: consultation responses, policy proposals;
- *not read* — a login-walled stub: the document's existence is public, its text is not.

Read on public pages and documents on 2026-11-02. Each entry distils at most five key
provisions in the vault's own words; the documents themselves are linked.

## AI as a passage in a wider instruction — listed, not filed

These public local documents are about something else, with AI as a short normative passage.
They are listed here rather than given files (read 2026-11-02):

* **GROWTH-AI for fetal-weight estimation** — a paragraph permitting an AI model as an equal alternative to the standard method, in three obstetric instructions: Herlev and Gentofte Hospital, *IUGR/FGR* and *Foetus magnus suspicio*; Rigshospitalet, *Intrauterin væksthæmning (FGR)* (Region Hovedstaden, VIP).
* **Standard for brystkræftscreening i Region Midtjylland** (Regionshospitalet Randers) — screening images read by one radiologist in combination with AI.
* **Lunge ventilation/perfusion SPECT-CT** (AUH Nuklearmedicin) — staff must check and correct the AI lung segmentation.
* **Udvælgelse af blastocyster** (Regionshospitalet Horsens, fertility) — an AI-based scoring system is part of embryo assessment.
* **KK 04.04.01 Good Tape — anvendelse** (Region Midtjylland, Koncern Kvalitet) — an AI-based transcription service, barred for sensitive data.
* **Håndtering af data på BBK** (Regionshospitalet Gødstrup, Blodprøver og Biokemi) — a section on which data the region's own chatbot may receive.
* **Information om e-Dok AI** (Region Midtjylland) — the document system's AI search summary should not be the sole basis for clinical decisions.
* **Brug af digital tolkeapp Helen Assistant** (Bispebjerg and Frederiksberg Hospital) and **Vejledning til brug af digital tolkeapp** (Herlev and Gentofte Hospital) — parallel to the [Nordsjællands Hospital guideline](../../policy/nordsjaellands-hospital-brug-af-digital-tolkeloesning-helen-assistant.md); their texts do not call the app AI.

* **Dansk Sygeplejeråd, *Teknologi – en del af fremtidens sygepleje*** — technology in general, with AI as an example; ***Teknologi og sygepleje*** in its members' knowledge bank is members-only and not titled as AI.
* Regional strategies, action plans and committee briefings that describe AI work without proposing rules or input on its use (Region Syddanmark, Region Midtjylland, Region Nordjylland, Region Hovedstaden, Region Sjælland).
* Articles in professional magazines (*Fysioterapeuten*, *Ergoterapeuten*), opinion pieces and newsletters.
* **FUTURE-AI: international consensus guideline for trustworthy and deployable artificial intelligence in healthcare** (FUTURE-AI Consortium, *BMJ* 2025;388:e081554, [doi:10.1136/bmj-2024-081554](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2024-081554); [future-ai.eu](https://future-ai.eu/)) — an international consensus guideline from 117 experts in 50 countries, funded under EU Horizon 2020 and built on six principles: fairness, universality, traceability, usability, robustness and explainability. Its issuer is not a Danish body, so it is not filed under the current admission rule; it would get a thin foreign-instrument stub once a Danish document in this register refers to it. No such reference is yet recorded in this vault (public pages searched 2026-11-13).
