# View — Generative-AI training for clinicians

A register of **generative-AI training and awareness material for clinicians and health
professionals**, offered by Danish suppliers — public, non-profit and commercial. Each entry
is one offering, with its scope, audience, specialty, level, delivery, time required, the
supplier's own update date, cost, access and status recorded in its `# Facts` table.

**Admission** (`CLAUDE.md` §1, the training-register carve-out): a Danish supplier; aimed at
clinicians or health professionals, generally or for a named specialty; and generative AI —
language models, ChatGPT, Copilot or the like — named within the offering's stated scope.
Material on AI in health that does not name generative AI is listed below as *read, not
filed*. Every entry was read on a public page or document on 2026-11-02; material behind a
staff login is recorded from its public description.

This register is the data layer for a planned website. Each line reads: supplier · form ·
delivery · time required · date.
