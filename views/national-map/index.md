# View — national map

The Danish landscape by sector and by funder. A lens, not a home.

## The shape of it

Danish public AI activity is organised along **two tracks with distinct origins**.

The **research track** is university-anchored: national consortia
([Pioneer](../../initiatives/pioneer-centre-for-ai.md), [CAISA](../../initiatives/caisa.md),
[ELLIS Unit Denmark](../../initiatives/ellis-unit-denmark.md)), faculty centres, and faculty
networks. It is funded by ministries and foundations and organised around disciplines.

The **delivery track** is government-anchored:
[Den Digitale Taskforce for Kunstig Intelligens](../../initiatives/digital-taskforce-kunstig-intelligens.md),
its [DKK 266.7m tripartite agreement](../../funding/offentlig-ai-aftale-2026.md), and
[KL's municipal network](../../initiatives/kl-netvaerk-for-kunstig-intelligens.md). It is
funded jointly by state, municipalities and regions, and organised around public-sector
adoption targets.

[Netværk for AI i Sundhed](../../initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md) connects to both:
its members come from universities, hospitals and authorities, and its
[think tank](../../initiatives/aisundhed-taenketank.md) names the Digital Taskforce among the
fora it contributes to.

## Possible further evolution

Patterns in the landscape suggest directions it could take:

- **Meeting points between the tracks.** The research consortia and the delivery bodies
  have separate funding and governance. Networks that draw members from both — as the
  national health-AI network does — are one way the tracks meet; shared events, advisory
  seats and joint projects are others.
- **The Digital Taskforce's expert group**, which draws on public-sector, business and
  research representatives, is a further setting in which the two tracks can inform each
  other.
- **2027-01-01** brings a new regional map — four regions, 17 health councils — and a
  merged national health-data organisation, which may open new forms of regional and
  national AI coordination.
