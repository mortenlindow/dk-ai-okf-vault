# View — AI in health

AI activity whose subject is health and care, assembled from every concept tagged `health`.
A lens, not a home: every concept below lives in its class folder.

Deployed clinical AI solutions are outside this vault's scope — they are mapped by the
[Nordic AI Health Map](../../references/nordic-ai-health-map.md).

## The shape of it

Danish public AI-in-health activity is organised at three levels, each with its own form.

- **National and cross-institutional.**
  [Netværk for AI i Sundhed](../../initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md), founded 2025,
  convenes clinicians, researchers, technology developers and decision-makers across
  hospitals, universities, companies and authorities. Its five working groups span needs
  assessment, cross-disciplinary learning, a think tank, implementation and the network's
  own operations. Membership grew from 173 to 270 in the week of its first general
  assembly in September 2026. [RAIN](../../initiatives/rain-network-ai-healthcare.md), an
  open network of about 500 professionals anchored in the Region of Southern Denmark, also
  draws members from across the country.
- **University faculties.** The
  [Centre for AI in Health](../../initiatives/centre-for-ai-in-health-aau.md) at Aalborg has a
  head, a deputy, a steering committee and rules of procedure, with Region Nordjylland as
  partner. The [Health-AI Network](../../initiatives/health-ai-network-au.md) at Aarhus is a
  co-chaired faculty network with open membership, holding its inaugural meeting in October
  2026. At UCPH, [CORS²](../../initiatives/cors.md) brings AI into drug-safety research.
- **Hospitals.** [CAI-X](../../initiatives/cai-x.md) joins Odense University Hospital with
  two SDU faculties. In the Capital Region, the
  [AI-Innovationsnetværket](../../initiatives/ai-innovationsnetvaerket-region-hovedstaden.md)
  (2019) connected hospitals across the region, and in 2020 gave rise to
  [RAIT](../../initiatives/rait.md), a radiology test centre spanning four hospitals.

Where founding dates are recorded they run from 2019 to 2026, and the bodies take a wide
range of organisational forms, from constituted centres to informal networks. The
earliest-dated bodies are hospital-anchored; the university networks are the most recent.

## Initiatives

### National

* [Netværk for AI i Sundhed](../../initiatives/netvaerk-for-ai-i-sundhed.md) - cross-institutional network, 2025. Its working groups:
  * [Behovsafdækning](../../initiatives/aisundhed-behovsafdaekning.md) - where AI can create the greatest clinical value.
  * [Læring på tværs](../../initiatives/aisundhed-laering-paa-tvaers.md) - competence building across professions and for patients.
  * [Tænketank](../../initiatives/aisundhed-taenketank.md) - the clinical voice in AI policy debate.
  * [Implementering](../../initiatives/aisundhed-implementering.md) - from pilot to sustained clinical operation.
  * [Sustainability](../../initiatives/aisundhed-sustainability.md) - the network's own operation and fundraising.

### Regional

* [RAIN – Network for AI in Healthcare](../../initiatives/rain-network-ai-healthcare.md) - open network of about 500 professionals, run by a Southern Denmark partnership that includes CAI-X.

### University-anchored

* [Centre for AI in Health (Aalborg University)](../../initiatives/centre-for-ai-in-health-aau.md) - faculty centre with a steering committee and rules of procedure.
* [Health-AI Network (Aarhus University)](../../initiatives/health-ai-network-au.md) - co-chaired faculty network with open membership.
* [CORS² — Copenhagen Centre for Regulatory Science](../../initiatives/cors.md) - UCPH pharmacy; AI in drug safety and pharmacovigilance.

### Hospital-anchored

* [CAI-X — Centre for Clinical Artificial Intelligence](../../initiatives/cai-x.md) - Odense University Hospital with SDU.
* [AI-Innovationsnetværket (Region Hovedstaden)](../../initiatives/ai-innovationsnetvaerket-region-hovedstaden.md) - informal cross-hospital network, 2019; recorded from its 2020 paper.
* [RAIT — Radiological Artificial Intelligence Test Centre](../../initiatives/rait.md) - four Capital Region radiology departments, 2020; grew out of the network above.

### Cross-sector

* [Netværk om AI (Danish Life Science Cluster)](../../initiatives/dlsc-netvaerk-om-ai.md) - public–private development network across the cluster's regional hubs.

## Possible further evolution

Patterns in the landscape suggest directions it could take:

- **Governance for the national network.** Having held its first general assembly, the
  network may publish statutes and an elected board — a natural step for a membership
  association of its size.
- **Connections across the levels.** The national network, the faculty bodies and the
  hospital bodies have largely separate origins. The think tank's link to national policy
  fora, and RAIT's origin in a regional network, show two ways such connections have formed.
- **Education.** The national network is exploring a master's programme for data scientists
  in health, alongside the adjacent machine-learning and health-informatics degrees at UCPH.
- **The 2027 reform.** On 2027-01-01 Region Hovedstaden and Region Sjælland merge into
  [Region Østdanmark](../../organisations/health/region-oestdanmark.md), 17 regional health
  councils begin, and Sundhedsdatastyrelsen, sundhed.dk and MedCom become
  [Digital Sundhed Danmark](../../organisations/health/digital-sundhed-danmark.md). The hospital
  layer of this view will sit in a new administrative setting.

For how this map itself is expected to grow, see [coverage](../coverage/index.md).
