# Coverage

**How far this map reaches, and how each part was read.** The frame below is the set of
bodies the vault is measured against; each entry records what was read and when. Check
here before starting a pass.

The vault began from four seed sources centred on UCPH and on AI in health. To keep the map
from simply following those sites' own links, the frame was set out *first*, and each sweep
is read against it.

Status words: **recorded** (written into the vault) · **read** (read on the date given) ·
**not yet read** · **outside the current rule** (read, and not included under this vault's
present scope rules — a statement about the rules, not about the body).

# Notes on sources

Practical observations from reading the landscape, for the next pass:

- **UCPH's university-wide centre table** (`forskning.ku.dk/omraader/centre/`) is the most
  complete single list of research centres, with 118 entries. UCPH's dedicated AI centres are
  presented on their own sites rather than in that table, so both are needed.
- **Hospital-anchored bodies** are best found by reading hospitals and regions directly;
  university pages do not describe them.
- **A centre's news archive** can carry details, such as founding dates, that its front page
  does not.
- **PDFs** — board papers, study regulations — are read with `pdftotext -layout`, and are
  sometimes a body's main public record.
- Some regional research surfaces use bot protection, and some sites rate-limit automated
  reading. Neither says anything about the bodies they describe; such pages are simply
  re-read later.

# Read, and outside the Denmark and Public/Non-profit tests

* **TRAIN Europe** — the Trustworthy and Responsible AI Network (read 2026-10-17, `traineurope.eu`). A European network of hospitals and partners, whose stated mission is to *"build a strong European network that unites AI pioneers and healthcare stakeholders to responsibly develop and implement AI technologies."* Its member list names Erasmus MC, UMC Utrecht, Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele, Helsinki University Hospital, Sahlgrenska / Region Västra Götaland, Region Skåne, Hospital Universitario Virgen del Rocío and Foundation 29, with KPMG and Microsoft as partners. With no Danish member as of this date, it sits outside the Denmark Test. Possible further evolution: a Danish member would bring it into scope as a thin foreign-body entry. It is a separate network from [RAIN](../../initiatives/rain-network-ai-healthcare.md).
* **Lex Futura** (read 2026-10-17, `lexfutura.eu`) — a Danish digital-compliance consultancy (A/S). As a company it sits outside the Public/Non-profit Test. Its pages name no partnership with an in-scope body. Unrelated companies use the same name in Switzerland and Finland, and a separate *LexFutura* runs an AI & Law summit.

# Open questions about this vault's rules

These concern how the vault draws its own boundaries, and are recorded for decision:

1. **AI as a method, or AI as an object of study.** SODAS applies machine learning; CIIR
   studies generative AI in law. The current rule treats both as outside scope. A vault about
   AI *organising* might include centres that study AI.
2. **Publication affiliations as evidence.** CSTAR appears as an institutional affiliation in
   peer-reviewed publications. Test 5 is framed around news items; affiliations are a
   different and more formal kind of record.
3. **Dated roles.** Where the only source for a leadership role is several years old, the
   vault records a dated governance row rather than a person file. `CLAUDE.md` could state
   this explicitly.
